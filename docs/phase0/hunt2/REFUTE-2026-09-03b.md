## Per-candidate notes

### 1. jhin-fiora-facebreaker-recall — SURVIVES (high)

Card text checked against `data/corpus_flat.txt`, all four lines exact:

```
UNL-022 | Jhin, Murderous Artist | Unit | Fury | E4 P1 M4 | [Deflect] ... [Ganking] ... When I move, [Add] :rb_energy_1::rb_rune_rainbow:.
SFD-180 | Fiora, Worthy | Unit | Order | E3 M3 | When a unit you control becomes [Mighty], you may pay :rb_rune_order: to ready it.
SFD-009 | Serrated Dirk | Gear | Fury | E1 M+0 | [Equip] :rb_rune_fury: [Effect] [Assault 2] (+2 :rb_might: while I'm an attacker.)
OGN-294 | Trifarian War Camp | Battlefield | Colorless | Units here have +1 :rb_might:. (This includes attackers.)
```

**Per-pass tally (Dirk + Eye version).** Resources in: +1 Energy, +1 rainbow Power (Jhin's move trigger). Resources out: 1 Order Power (Fiora). Cards in/out: zero — nothing leaves hand after the first pass. Bodies: +1 Recruit token, recalled to base exhausted. **Net: +1 Energy, ±0 Power, +1 Recruit, unbounded.**

**Why every step is legal.**

- Standard Move base → battlefield: 144.4.a; cost is exhausting the unit (144.2). No per-turn cap on Standard Moves — the only limiter is the exhaust, which Fiora refunds.
- Jhin gains the Attacker designation at 464.2.c.3 ("Units at the Contested Battlefield controlled by the Attacker … gain the Attacker … designation now"), so Assault 2 turns on there: 4 → 6. 709: *"A Unit 'becomes Mighty' at the moment its Might changes from being less than 5 to being 5 or greater."* This is a literal crossing, not a strained reading — **R13 is directly supported by 709 for the Assault case**; the only thing a human still has to bless is whether an aura/battlefield bonus counts the same way (it reads identically).
- The Recruit token from Eye of the Herald is played at the battlefield *before* combat opens, so it is present at 464.2.c.3 and takes the Attacker designation too — it is recalled with Jhin, not stranded. (Had it stayed, 466.3.d.1 would re-stage a Showdown and Combat every pass and the turn would never advance.)
- 466.1.a.1 inserts *"3c. Heal all Units"* and 466.1.a.2 inserts *"3d. Recall Attackers present at the Battlefield if Defenders are still present."* Both are unconditional in a combat cleanup, so the Recruit's 1 damage never accumulates on the defender and Jhin never wins the combat.
- Recall is not a Move: **456** *"Recalls are not Moves."* **456.1** *"They do not cause Triggered Abilities to trigger that are triggered by Move actions."* This is the exact rule the entry's note gestures at (it cites 458/458.1, which are the right rules for *state*, but 456/456.1 is the right rule for *not a move*). So no second Energy, no second Recruit on the way home — the entry's "+1 −1 per pass, not infinite Power" claim is right for the right reason.
- Jhin comes home ready because Fiora readied him at the battlefield and **458.1** *"Damage and statuses of a permanent will all remain unaffected by a Recall."* Same rule keeps the stun on him: 423.1.a.2 says stun is only cleared *"during step 3d of the end of turn cleanup"* — the end-of-**turn** cleanup, not the combat cleanup. One Facebreaker does cover the whole turn.
- After 3d the attacker has no units at the battlefield, so 466.3.d.1's *"both players have units remaining"* is false and no fresh combat is staged. 466.5.a clears Contested, so the next pass re-contests cleanly.
- **The defender cannot walk away.** A Standard Move can only be made during its controller's own Main Phase (144.1.a) and never during a Showdown or Combat (144.1.c). The opponent is locked into the loop unless they hold a removal/move effect.
- Facebreaker: `[Hidden] … [Action] … Stun a friendly unit and an enemy unit at the same battlefield.` Hidden cost is 1 rainbow to hide, 0 Energy to play (the entry's note quotes the printed 2 Energy, which is the hard-cast price — not wrong, just not the price actually paid). It must be played once Jhin is already at the battlefield ("at the same battlefield"), i.e. in the first Combat Showdown, which is what the entry says.
- Power source: Jhin's `[Add] rainbow` resolves on the chain from the move, well before Fiora's trigger at attacker designation, and 167 only empties the Rune Pool at the start of a Main Phase and end of turn — the float survives across passes inside the turn. No 429.3 gymnastics needed in the Dirk version.
- Domain identity 103.1.b: the line is Fury (Jhin, Dirk) + Order (Fiora, Facebreaker, Eye). Three legends cover it — OGN-253 Hand of Noxus, SFD-187 Void Burrower, UNL-187 Piltover Enforcer. All three named finishers are legal too: SFD-177 Azir, Sovereign (Order), UNL-157 Scrutinizing Sergeant (Order), OGN-293 The Grand Plaza (Colorless battlefield).

**Two holes the entry does not cover.**

1. **466.5.d gives the opponent a Conquer on pass 1** if they did not already control the battlefield: *"Establishing Control results in a Conquer if that player has not yet scored this Battlefield this turn."* After the recall the defender is the only player with units there, so they Establish Control and score once. Only once (they control it from then on), but on a board where they are at 7 points that single point ends the game. The precondition must say the battlefield is **already theirs**.
2. **Jhin can die.** Facebreaker stuns exactly one enemy unit. Any other enemy unit at that battlefield still deals combat damage, and 143.2.a kills at damage ≥ Might *before* 3c heals. So the un-stunned enemy Might at the battlefield must total < 6 (< 5 for the War-Camp-only build, where Jhin is 5). Symmetrically, un-stunned enemy damage kills the 1-Might Recruit each pass, which does not stop the Energy loop but does stop the Recruit accumulation.

**Three-card floor at Trifarian War Camp — holds, with one wrinkle.** Jhin 4 + War Camp 1 = 5, a real crossing every pass, and no Equipment needed. But here Fiora's trigger and Jhin's `[Add]` both become pending off the *same* move, so the Power is not yet in the pool when Fiora's item is created. Harmless: the controller chooses the order their own simultaneous triggers go on the chain (808.2.a *"The controller will choose the order to add these Triggers to the chain"*), so order them to resolve Add-first; failing that, 429.3 lets you recycle a rune for Power mid-payment. Also flag the format dependence per spec item 23: three battlefields brought, one used — random in Duel (485.5), chosen in Match (486.5), so the War Camp floor is a Match-format claim.

**PROPOSED EDIT — `prerequisites.notable`** (replace the first bullet):

> An enemy unit of 2+ Might defends a battlefield the opponent **already controls**, and the total Might of enemy units there that Facebreaker does not stun is less than Jhin's attacking Might. The first matters because after the recall the defender is the only player with units at the battlefield, and 466.5.d turns Establishing Control into a Conquer — one free point for the opponent if the battlefield was not already theirs. The second matters because Facebreaker stuns exactly one enemy unit and damage kills at 143.2.a before the cleanup heals in 3c. Jhin ready in base with the Assault source; Fiora in base.

**PROPOSED EDIT — second `prerequisites.notable` bullet** (rule citations):

> RULES the loop stands on: the combat cleanup heals all units (466.1.a.1) and recalls the attackers when defenders remain (466.1.a.2), leaving No Result (466.3.d) with no re-staged combat because only one player has units left (466.3.d.1). A recall is explicitly not a move — "Recalls are not Moves… They do not cause Triggered Abilities to trigger that are triggered by Move actions" (456, 456.1) — so Jhin does not re-trigger on the way home, and 458.1 keeps him ready and stunned. The stun lasts the whole turn: it is cleared only in step 3d of the end-of-turn cleanup (423.1.a.2), not in the combat cleanup. The defender cannot escape: a Standard Move happens only in its controller's own Main Phase (144.1.a) and never during a Showdown or Combat (144.1.c). R13: 709 defines "becomes Mighty" as the moment Might crosses from below 5 to 5+, and Assault switches on with the Attacker designation at 464.2.c.3, so the crossing is fresh every pass.

**PROPOSED EDIT — `notes`, replace the third sentence** ("Three-card floor…"):

> Three-card floor: Jhin + Fiora + Facebreaker at Trifarian War Camp is infinite Energy with no Equipment — but only in Match, where you pick which of your three battlefields is used (486.5); in Duel it is random (485.5). In that build Fiora's trigger and Jhin's Add come off the same move, so put them on the chain in the order that resolves the Add first (808.2.a).

### 2. garen-fiora-malzahar-facebreaker-recruits — WOUNDED (high)

Card text checked; all exact:

```
OGS-013 | Garen, Commander | Unit | Order | E6 P1 M5 | Other friendly units have +1 :rb_might: here.
OGN-113 | Malzahar, Fanatic | Unit | Mind | E4 M3 | Kill a friendly unit or gear, :rb_exhaust:: [Action] — [Add] :rb_rune_rainbow::rb_rune_rainbow:.
OGN-095 | Stupefy | Spell | Mind | E1 | [Reaction] Give a unit -1 :rb_might: this turn, to a minimum of 1 :rb_might:. Draw 1.
SFD-153 | Eye of the Herald | Gear | Order | E1 M+0 | [Equip] :rb_rune_order: [Effect] When I move, play a 1 :rb_might: Recruit unit token here.
```

**Per-pass tally (War Camp version).** Power out: 1 Order (Fiora readies Garen on the way in) + 1 Order (Fiora readies Malzahar on the way home) = 2. Power in: 2 rainbow (Malzahar's Add). **Net Power 0.** Energy: 0 in, 0 out. Cards: 0 in, 0 out. Bodies: 2 Recruits enter at the battlefield, Malzahar eats 1 as his cost, the survivor is an attacker and is recalled to base — **net +1 Recruit in base per pass.** Unbounded, engine-only.

**What holds.**

- Two Eyes on one unit is legal: 718.5.d only forbids one attached card being on two hosts (*"A card may be Attached only to a single Top-Most card at a time"*), not two Equipment on one host. Two independent granted triggers, two Recruits per move.
- Garen's aura is location-scoped ("…here"), so it toggles Malzahar between 4 and 5 as Garen leaves and returns. That toggling is exactly what makes the crossing repeatable (709).
- Malzahar's ability is `[Action]`, so it is usable in the Combat Showdown, and its exhaust cost is refunded by the Fiora trigger that fires at the recall in the same cleanup. Killing a Recruit token as a cost is fine — a token is a unit.
- The Recruits gain the Attacker designation at 464.2.c.3 (present before combat opened), so the survivor comes home rather than stranding the loop in the 466.3.d.1 re-stage.
- Domain identity: Order (Garen, Fiora, Facebreaker, Eye of the Herald) + Mind (Malzahar, Stupefy). Four Mind/Order legends exist — OGN-265 Herald of the Arcane, OGS-021 Lady of Luminosity - Starter, SFD-201 Chem-Baroness, UNL-199 Deceiver.
- Stupefy is a one-off, not per pass: "-1 Might **this turn**" persists for the whole loop, and Garen's own aura says "**Other** friendly units", so it never lifts him back over the line. 5 − 1 = 4 in base, 4 + 1 = 5 at the War Camp: a clean crossing every pass.

**Three defects.**

1. **The Malzahar buff is unnamed, and the named candidate is the wrong card.** Malzahar 3 + Garen's aura 1 = 4 — this is precisely the 2026-08-30 calibration failure the REFUTE spec is built around, and the entry only escapes it via "Rally the Troops or any buff". Rally the Troops (SFD-166) reads *"When a friendly unit is played this turn, buff it"* — it buffs units **played after it resolves**, so Malzahar would have to be played that turn, and 143.4 (**R11**) makes him enter exhausted, so pass 1 gets no Add and the priming requirement doubles. The correct card is **Bonds of Strength SFD-151** (Order, 2 Energy, `[Reaction] Give two friendly units each +1 Might this turn`), which buffs a Malzahar already on the board. Do not point the second +1 at Garen: 5 − 1 + 1 = 5 in base makes him permanently Mighty and kills his crossing.
2. **The loop needs 1 Power of priming and the entry claims "Power in equals Power out".** Within a pass the order is: Fiora pays 1 (Garen's crossing on the way in) → Malzahar adds 2 (showdown) → Fiora pays 1 (Malzahar's crossing at the recall). The first payment precedes the pass's income, so the pool must start the loop at 1 Power. Steady state per pass is indeed 0, but the entry's step 6 as written implies no priming.
3. **NEW READING / hard bug: the Darius variant and the Malzahar buff cannot be combined.** OGN-243 Darius, Executioner is Order (`E6 P1 M6 | [Legion] … Other friendly units have +1 :rb_might: here.`), so the swap is domain-legal — good. But with Darius in base *and* Bonds of Strength on Malzahar, Malzahar is 3 + 1 + 1 = 5 with Garen away and 6 with Garen home: he never falls below 5, never re-crosses (709), Fiora never readies him, and the loop stops after one pass. The Darius line works only **without** the Malzahar buff: then Malzahar is 4 in base with Garen away, 5 with Garen home (crossing), and Garen — Stupefied to 4 — is 4 at the battlefield and 5 in base with Darius (crossing). Both cross simultaneously at the recall, both are readied, and **no priming is needed** because Malzahar's +2 lands before either payment. That is strictly the better build; it just is not the one the entry describes ("then Garen crosses on the way home instead" is right, but it silently drops the Malzahar buff).

**Also worth carrying over from entry 1** (the entry has the same two holes and does not mention either): 466.5.d gives the defender a Conquer on pass 1 unless they already control the battlefield; and the un-stunned enemy Might at that battlefield must be low enough that Garen (4) and the surviving Recruit (1) are not killed in the damage step, since 143.2.a kills before 3c heals. The stated "4+ Might defender" is over-tight in one direction (2+ suffices to survive the single Recruit's damage) and silent in the other (a second, un-stunned defender kills Garen).

**PROPOSED EDIT — `uses` note for OGN-113:**

> 3 Might plus a +1 buff = 4; Garen's aura in base makes him 5 and Mighty. Kill a friendly unit or gear, exhaust: Add 2 rainbow — eats one Recruit per pass and pays both Fiora readies. The buff must come from a Mind or Order card that hits a unit already on the board: Bonds of Strength (SFD-151, 2 Energy Reaction, +1 Might this turn to two friendly units). Rally the Troops only buffs units played after it resolves, which would force Malzahar to be replayed and enter exhausted (143.4).

**PROPOSED EDIT — `prerequisites.notable`, first bullet:**

> The opponent defends Trifarian War Camp (OGN-294, +1 Might to units there) with a unit they already control the battlefield with, of 2+ Might, and with no second un-stunned unit there — after the recall the defender Establishes Control, which is a Conquer worth a point if the battlefield was not already theirs (466.5.d), and any un-stunned defender damage kills Garen or the Recruit before the cleanup heals (143.2.a vs 466.1.a.1). Malzahar enters the loop buffed by Bonds of Strength; the loop must be primed with 1 Power, because Fiora's ready on the way in is paid before Malzahar's Add that pass. Darius, Executioner (OGN-243, Order, +1 Might to other friendly units here) can replace the War Camp — but then drop the Malzahar buff, or he sits at 5 with Garen away and never re-crosses. In the Darius build both Garen and Malzahar cross on the way home, Malzahar's +2 lands first, and no priming is needed.

**PROPOSED EDIT — `steps`, replace step 6:**

> Repeat from the attack: 2 Power in, 2 Power out, and one Recruit stays behind in base every pass. The loop needs 1 Power of priming in the War Camp build, because the first Fiora payment happens before Malzahar's Add.

### 3. jhin-virtuoso-ekko-malzahar-vi — WOUNDED (high)

Card text checked; every quoted cost line in the entry is exact. Three text points the entry gets right and one it gets wrong:

```
UNL-181 | Virtuoso | Legend | Fury/Mind | When you play a spell, if you spent :rb_energy_4: or more, you may banish it. Then, if there are four spells banished with me, put each in its trash, channel 4 runes, and draw 1.
OGN-036 | Vi, Destructive | Unit | Fury | E2 P1 M3 | [Ganking] Recycle 1 from your trash: Give me +1 :rb_might: this turn.
SFD-088 | Renata Glasc, Mastermind | Unit | Mind | E5 M4 | :rb_energy_1::rb_rune_mind:: Draw 1. :rb_energy_4: + 4x:rb_rune_mind:, :rb_exhaust:: Score 1 point. Use my abilities only while I'm at a battlefield.
```

"channels 4 runes (**ready**)" is right — 430.2.a: *"By default, runes are channeled readied."* Domain identity is clean: Fury (Vi, Upstage Comedy, Brynhir) + Mind (Ekko, Malzahar, Consult the Past, Progress Day, Renata) under Virtuoso, Fury/Mind.

**Recycle order is deterministic — the parent's question answered.** 416.5 randomises only *simultaneous* recycles (*"If 2 or more cards are Recycled to the Main Deck simultaneously, they are placed on the bottom of that deck in a random order"*). Vi's cost is `Recycle 1`, one card per activation, and Ekko's Deathknell recycles one card. So with an empty deck the order is fully known: Ekko's Deathknell first puts Ekko on the bottom, then Vi's activation puts Upstage Comedy below him, and the Draw 2 takes Ekko then Comedy. **This is exactly what killed L3-05** (Garbage Grabber's `Recycle 3` is simultaneous → 1/3 chance the key card is on top); Vi is immune to that refutation.

**Exact per-pass ledger, Draw-2 cycle, R runes on the board.**

| | Energy | Power | cards out of hand | cards recycled | draws |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ekko OGN-110 | −5 | −1 Mind | Ekko | Ekko (Deathknell) | |
| Upstage Comedy UNL-009 | −2 | | Comedy | Comedy (Vi, free) | |
| Malzahar OGN-113 kill+exhaust | | +2 rainbow | | | |
| Consult the Past OGN-083 | −4 | | Consult (banished) | — | +2 |
| Ekko Deathknell readies runes | +R | | | | |
| **net** | **R − 11** | **+1** | 3 | 2 | 2 |

Rainbow is Universal Power (163.2.b), so Malzahar's +2 legally pays Ekko's Mind cost. Because it does, **no rune has to be recycled after the first pass**, which is what keeps R at its maximum — the parent's worry ("runes recycled for Power that turn are back in the Rune Deck, not readied", 161.2.b) is real but the loop dodges it from pass 2 onward. Only the very first Ekko needs a rune recycled, and Virtuoso's first channel puts that rune back.

**So the loop is break-even in Energy at 11 runes and +1 Energy at 12.** That makes "12 runes on the board" a hard precondition, not a flourish. The entry's cost lines (11E+1P and 13E+2P) are exact, but it never states the net, and the net is thin.

**Progress Day cycle is Energy-negative.** 5E+1P (Ekko) + 2E (Comedy) + 6E+1P (Progress Day) = 13E + 2P against 12E + 2P income: **−1 Energy, ±0 Power, +2 spare draws.** It is a draw pump, not an engine, and the entry presents the two cycles as interchangeable.

**R7 — the draw budget, which the entry does not mention.** Per Draw-2 pass, exactly 2 cards need to come back through the deck (Ekko, Comedy) and Consult draws exactly 2. Balanced, and the deck holds exactly 2 when the draw happens, so no Burn Out (431.2). The debt lands on the fourth pass: Virtuoso's *"put each in its trash"* dumps four spent draw spells into the trash at once. Vi can recycle them for free, but they then need **four draws that the cycle does not produce** — Virtuoso's own `draw 1` covers one; the other three cost Renata `1 Energy + 1 Mind Power: Draw 1` each, i.e. **3 Energy + 3 Mind Power per four passes**. (A fourth is needed in practice, or one spell is left buried and the deck stops being empty at the top of each pass, which breaks the "recycled card is the next draw" guarantee.)

**The entry misses the single biggest gain: rune churn against Virtuoso's channel.** With 12 runes already on the board the Rune Deck is empty and `channel 4` does nothing (430.3: *"If there aren't sufficient runes in the Rune Deck, channel as many as possible"*). But a rune's two abilities are independent — 164.2.a `[E]: Add 1` and 164.2.b `Recycle this: Add [Power]`, with no exhaust in the recycle cost — so an **already-exhausted** rune can still be recycled for Power. Recycle four spent runes just before the fourth banish resolves: **+4 Power**, board drops to 8, Rune Deck holds 4, and Virtuoso then channels them back **ready** for **+4 Energy**. Free, once per supercycle.

**Corrected supercycle (4 Draw-2 passes, 12 runes):** +4 Energy +4 Power from the passes, +4 Energy +4 Power from the rune churn, −4 Energy −4 Mind Power to redraw the four returned spells. **Net +4 Energy +4 Power per four passes, unbounded.** Renata converts at 4 Energy + 4 Mind Power per point, plus the 2 Energy Repeat on Upstage Comedy that readies her alongside Malzahar (820.1.c.3 caps Repeat at one extra execution, so one Comedy readies exactly two units). Eight points is therefore reachable but slow — on the order of forty passes — and the whole thing must fit in one turn, because 167 empties the Rune Pool at end of turn.

**What changed versus L3-08 (docs/phase0/hunt2/L3-ready-resource.md:71–76).** L3-08's Mind/Chaos tally died on a Power deficit of 8 per cycle with the explicit note that *"Malzahar's +2P is once per ready"*; its Calm/Mind tally died at +2E −2P with *"no recursion"*. Three things this route adds that none of those had: (1) a **repeatable ready for Malzahar** — Upstage Comedy is Fury, in-domain under Virtuoso, 2 Energy, and Vi recycles it for free, so the +2 rainbow is once per *pass*, not once per turn; (2) **Vi's free unlimited recycle** replaces Forge of the Future, costing neither Energy nor a card slot, and being single-card it escapes 416.5's randomisation; (3) **Virtuoso's banish** removes the draw spell from the recycle budget for four passes and, with the churn above, converts spent runes into ready ones. The L3-08 verdicts stand for the decks they were tallied in; this is a different resource base, not a contradiction.

**Two smaller corrections.** Consult the Past played from `[Hidden]` costs 0 Energy, which is *below* Virtuoso's 4-Energy threshold — it must be hard-cast for the banish to trigger. And Brynhir Thundersong is a 6 Energy 5 Might unit whose protection is a play trigger, so it is a one-off tax on the combo turn, not part of the loop.

**PROPOSED EDIT — `prerequisites.notable`, replace the second bullet:**

> Cost lines per pass: the Draw-2 cycle spends 11 Energy + 1 Power (Ekko 5E+1P, Upstage Comedy 2E, the 4-Energy draw spell) and takes back one full readying of every rune on the board plus Malzahar's 2 rainbow. Because rainbow is Universal Power (163.2.b) it pays Ekko's Mind cost, so no rune is recycled after the first pass and the board stays full. That makes the net **(runes on board − 11) Energy and +1 Power per pass**: break-even at 11 runes, +1 Energy at 12. Twelve runes on the board is a hard precondition. The Progress Day cycle spends 13 Energy + 2 Power for 12 Energy + 2 Power, so it is **−1 Energy** — a draw pump for the passes that need spare draws, not an engine.

### 4 virtuoso-banish-channel
Corpus line, exact:
> `UNL-181 | Virtuoso | Legend | Fury/Mind | - | When you play a spell, if you spent :rb_energy_4: or more, you may banish it. Then, if there are four spells banished with me, put each in its trash, channel 4 runes, and draw 1.`

Everything the entry's `notable` says about resources is right: channel obeys the Rune Deck (161.2.a *"Exactly 12 Rune cards"*, 430.3 *"If there aren't sufficient runes in the Rune Deck, channel as many as possible"*), and the runes arrive ready because Virtuoso does not say otherwise (430.2.a *"By default, runes are channeled readied"*).

**The blocker is R26, and it is structural.** 340.1: *"The newest Finalized Chain Item resolves."* A "when you play a spell" trigger is appended to the Chain after the spell has become a Finalized Chain Item (359.3.a) and is therefore *newer*, so it resolves **first** — while the spell is still sitting on the Chain unresolved. 359.3.d is explicit that a spell only reaches the trash after *"execute the game effect of the spell"*. So on a literal read, "you may banish it" pulls the spell off the Chain before it does anything: Virtuoso counters your own 4-Energy spell, four times, to channel 4 runes and draw 1. The entry's own `notes` ("The four spells return to the trash, so anything that recycles or replays from trash … can loop them") silently assumes the opposite ordering. This needs a human ruling before the entry can stand.

**Numbers.** Even under the favourable reading the payoff is smaller than "+4 Energy and a card per four spells": the four runes are *permanent* board ramp toward a hard ceiling of 12, so the engine's whole lifetime output is (12 − runes you would have channelled anyway) plus one card per four big spells, and four spells at 4+ Energy each is 16+ Energy — two full turns at 12 runes. After the Rune Deck empties the cycle yields only "draw 1".

**Class.** `uses` contains exactly one card and it is the legend's own printed text; there is no interaction partner. Whether that belongs in a *combo* catalogue is a call for the human, not a rules verdict — flagging it, not refuting it.

**PROPOSED EDIT**

`prerequisites.notable`, append →
> Unruled (R26): the Chain resolves newest-first (340.1) and a "when you play a spell" trigger is appended after the spell, so a literal read has Virtuoso's trigger resolve while the spell is still an unresolved Chain Item — banishing it there would counter your own spell (359.3.d only sends a spell to the trash after its effect executes). The entry assumes the trigger waits until the spell has resolved.

`terminatesIn` →
> engine only — four spells of 4+ Energy convert into 4 ready runes and a card, but the runes are permanent ramp against a 12-rune ceiling (161.2.a), so the lifetime output is at most the runes you had not already channelled plus one card per 16+ Energy of spells; worthless once all 12 are out

### 5 dazzling-aurora-elder-dragon
Card text exact for both, and the errata citation checks out: `data/errata.json` has Dazzling Aurora effective **2025-10-28**, `until you reveal a unit. Play it,` → `until you reveal a unit and banish it. Play it,`.

**"Board wipe" is the error, and it is in the entry's `name`.** 198.1: *"Locations include the Battlefields and the Bases."* 485.4 / 486.4: sanctioned 1v1 has **Battlefield Count: 2** (each player brings three and one each is used, 485.5). So the locations that can hold enemy units are 2 battlefields + the enemy base = **3**, and Elder Dragon's *"choose up to one enemy unit at each location. Deal 1 to them"* kills at most **three** enemy units — one per location, leaving everything else standing. Against a wide board (six Recruits at a battlefield) it kills exactly one of them. Thinning to almost no units to raise the Dragon hit rate makes this worse, which is the tension in the entry's "deck-building lever".

### 6 leblanc-zilean-reflection-doubling
Card text exact for all five, and both errata citations are right (`Keeper of Masks` and `Mirror Image` both gained "Then do this:" on **2026-04-03**).

**The recursion is confirmed, and the entry's N+1 is the correct number** — not 2^N. 371.1: *"These Replacement Effects [once each turn] may only be applied to the specified number of **events** each turn."* Zilean #1 turns one token play into two token events; Zilean #2 may apply to exactly one of them, giving three; Zilean #3 gives four. So N Zileans at that battlefield yield **N+1** tokens from a single Reflection play, and the pilot's "doubles then adds 1" (Z → 2Z+1) is right.

**The copies really do become Zileans.** 375's third example is exactly this shape: *"A spell reads 'play a ready 3 [M] Mech token. Then do this: Give it Temporary.' A Replacement Effect that says 'if you would play a unit token, play that token and a 1 [M] Recruit token instead' is applied to the event of the Mech token being made. The Recruit token enters ready and is given Temporary."* So Deceiver's "Then do this: It becomes a copy of another unit there. Give it [Temporary]" rides onto every extra token Zilean produced. And 477.1.b.1.a puts **Rules Text** in the copyable traits, so each clone carries Zilean's own once-each-turn replacement — and each is a new object, so each has an unused once-each-turn for the *next* token play this turn.

**Error 1 — Mirror Image's batch is unprotected and does not compound.** UNL-200 reads *"Play a ready Reflection unit token **to your base**."* LeBlanc, Everywhere at Once only suppresses *"Your [Temporary] effects at **my battlefield**"*, so a base-side Reflection dies at the start of your Beginning Phase (816.1.b) unless it walks to her battlefield first. They enter ready, so a Standard Move (144.4.a) fixes it for free — but the step has to be in the entry. Until they move, they are not "at a battlefield", so they do not add to the next score's multiplier either.

**Error 2 — growth is once per turn.** Deceiver's ability costs *"discard 1 and exhaust me"*; the legend readies only in the Awaken Phase (315.1.b), so at most one Reflection play per turn no matter how many battlefields you hold, and 470 caps scoring at once per battlefield per turn anyway. Starting from the one hard-cast Zilean the ceiling is 1 → 3 → 7 → 15 → 31 → 63 → 127, i.e. the reported 71 needs five to six consecutive uncontested scoring turns with LeBlanc alive, one discard each.

**New reading R27 — do the clones have Might?** 477.1.b.1.a lists the copyable traits as *Name, Super Type, Type, Tags, Cost, Domain, Rules Text*. **Might is not on that list** (477.1.a.1 handles Might assignment separately in the same layer). Read strictly, a 0 [M] Reflection token (187.6) that becomes a copy of Zilean is a 0-Might unit carrying Zilean's rules text. That does not touch the engine — the doubling needs only the rules text — but it guts the payoff, and it is the reason the entry's Darius line exists. This applies to every Reflection entry in the database, not just this one.

**PROPOSED EDIT**

`steps[1]` →
> Next score: every Zilean at that battlefield doubles the token again. Mirror Image adds another batch, but it plays to your **base** — those Reflections enter ready, so Standard-Move each of them to LeBlanc's battlefield the same turn or they die at your Beginning Phase (816.1.b), and until they arrive they do not count toward the next multiplier.

`prerequisites.notable[0]`, append →
> Deceiver exhausts to make the Reflection and only readies in your Awaken, so this fires **once per turn** whatever you score. From one hard-cast Zilean the ceiling is 3, 7, 15, 31, 63, 127 — the reported 71 is five to six consecutive uncontested scoring turns with a discard each.

`prerequisites.notable`, append →
> Unruled (R27): Might is not among the copyable traits in 477.1.b.1.a, so the clones may all be 0 Might carrying Zilean's rules text. The engine survives either way — the doubling only needs the text — but the board that comes out of it does not.

## Shared readings

Existing readings applied without re-litigating: **R1**, **R2**, **R9** (a "When I move" trigger fires on effect moves and "here" is the post-move location — load-bearing for treasure-hunter-industrialist-gold), **R10** (LeBlanc suppresses the Temporary kill trigger — load-bearing for leblanc-zilean-reflection-doubling), **R11** (units enter exhausted unless text overrides — load-bearing for the first-turn output of treasure-hunter and fae-dragon, harmless for dazzling-aurora and pack-of-wonders).

Three new readings, numbered from R25 as instructed.

**R25 — "Your tokens enter ready" (Renata Glasc, Industrialist, SFD-171) versus a creating effect that says "play a Gold gear token *exhausted*".** Decides `treasure-hunter-industrialist-gold` and `fae-dragon-wallop-industrialist`, and would touch every one of the 18 Gold-makers in the pool.

*Reading A (the entries' reading — the Gold enters ready).* Renata's text describes how a token enters, which 369.3 identifies as a replacement effect (*"Master Yi, Honed reads 'I enter ready.' This applies a replacement effect to the way that units normally enter"*). 370.1.c applies replacement effects *"before any qualifying event has actually occurred"*, so Renata rewrites the entering event, exhausted rider and all. Under this reading each Gold is 1 rainbow Power the moment it appears (187.5's cost is `Kill this, [exhaust]`, so ready is the whole difference between "Power now" and "Power next turn").

*Reading B (exhausted wins).* 184.1 gives the creating effect the authority to set the state: *"The effect may state that the token enters ready or exhausted, if that state is contrary to the default for the token's type"* — gear default to ready (149.1), so "exhausted" is a deliberate, specific override. 375 then makes that rider sticky, and its own worked example is **Treasure Hunter by name**: *"Treasure Hunter reads 'When I move, play a Gold gear token exhausted.' A Replacement Effect … is applied to the event of the Gold gear token being played. The additional copy will also be exhausted, as it inherits the 'exhausted' modification."* If a replacement applying to that event inherits the modification, Renata's inherits it too and the token still enters exhausted.

*What separates them:* 375's examples all concern riders passed to **new objects created by the replacement** (an extra copy, a substituted Recruit), never a replacement whose own instruction contradicts the rider. Nothing in the rules resolves the direct contradiction. 002 does not help — both sides are card text. Note also that this is **not** the reading the verified Stealthy Pursuer / Eye of the Herald line depends on: Eye of the Herald says only *"play a 1 [M] Recruit unit token here"*, so Renata there overrides the type **default** (143.4) with no card text to fight, which is uncontroversial. Renata is the only "your tokens enter ready" card in the pool, and Gold are the only tokens printed "exhausted", so R25 is exactly this one matchup.

**R26 — does a "when you play a spell" trigger resolve before or after the spell?** Decides `virtuoso-banish-channel`.

*Reading A (before — the literal read).* The spell becomes a Finalized Chain Item at step 6 of playing (359.3.a); the trigger's condition is evaluated *"after a potentially inciting event has been processed"* (383.2.c) and is appended to the Chain after it; 340.1 resolves *"The newest Finalized Chain Item"* first. So Virtuoso's trigger resolves while the spell is still unresolved on the Chain, and "you may banish it" removes it before it ever executes — 359.3.d only sends a spell to the trash *after* *"execute the game effect of the spell"*. Virtuoso would counter your own spells.

*Reading B (after — the design read).* Riot's word for removing a spell from the Chain is "Counter" (Defy: *"Counter a spell that costs no more than [4]"*), not "banish"; and Virtuoso's own follow-up, *"put each in its trash"*, only makes sense for spells that finished and were pulled out of the trash. This requires treating the trigger as waiting for the spell to resolve, which no rule in the file states.

Fizz, Trickster (SFD-140, *"play a spell from your trash … **Then recycle it**"*) has the same shape and would be equally self-defeating under Reading A, which is circumstantial support for Reading B but not a rule.

**R27 — is Might a copyable trait?** Decides the payoff (not the engine) of `leblanc-zilean-reflection-doubling`, and every other Reflection/copy entry in the database.

*Reading A (clones keep 0 Might).* 477.1.b.1.a enumerates the copyable traits as *Name, Super Type, Type, Tags, Cost, Domain, Rules Text* — Might is absent, and 477.1.a.1 treats *"Assignment of Might"* as a separate matter in the same layer. A 0 [M] Reflection token (187.6) that becomes a copy of Zilean is then a 0-Might unit carrying Zilean's rules text.

*Reading B (clones get the copied Might).* The enumeration is an oversight; every published example of the archetype (and the entry's own Darius line, which exists to hand the clones Might) assumes real bodies. 185.3.a.2's example already shows Deceiver's copy effect appending *"all copyable traits, including the cost of the unit to be copied"* to a Reflection, treating the copy as a full stand-in.

Either way the doubling engine works — it needs only the Rules Text, which *is* on the list.
