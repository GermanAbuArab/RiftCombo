## Method

Every quoted `note` was checked against `data/corpus_flat.txt` by card code; every legend pair against the 49-legend list in the corpus; rule numbers against `docs/phase0/rules-primer.md`. Card text mismatches found: none that change a line (the Gauntlets "free" claim in §12 and the "Heimerdinger" stray note in §19 are annotation errors, not text errors). Domain identity: every entry names a legend that exists; the two "only legend" claims (VEN-149 Mind/Body, VEN-139 Fury/Calm) are correct, and VEN-155 is indeed the only Order/Chaos legend.

## Per-candidate notes (only what the table does not say)

### 1 ahri-blue-sentinel-hold
Text exact. Calm/Mind: OGN-255, SFD-189, UNL-189, VEN-145. Attack on R1 is the only attack; arithmetic holds. Realistic cost is not the cards but surviving the opponent's turn with 4 units on one battlefield (Ahri M4, Sentinel M6 defending).

### 2 tryndamere-brambleback-conquer
"When I conquer **after an attack**, if you assigned 5 or more excess damage to enemy units" — an uncontested walk-in is a Conquer (466.5.d) but not an attack with assigned damage, so a garrison must be present and must total ≤ (attackers' Might − 5). Entry's "1 + K times" is per Tryndamere; with one Tryndamere and two Bramblebacks that is 4 points, a strong Conquer, not a BURST to 8. It only reaches 8 with two Tryndameres (14E+4P on top of the Bramblebacks).

### 3 grand-plaza-recruit-vanguard
Recruit the Vanguard plays tokens "to your base or to battlefields you control" — the Plaza must already be yours. Two Vanguards in one turn is 12E, the whole Rune Deck. Combat maths on the opponent's turn: a lone 6-Might attacker kills six 1-Might Recruits and dies to the remaining damage; two Recruits stay, no win.

### 4 gutter-palace
"exactly 4 cards in hand" is measured at the start of your Beginning Phase, before your draw. Palace's Bird ability adjusts both counts at once (−1 hand, +1 unit at a battlefield you control). The note's "Temporary units do not count" is not certain: 816.1.b and the Palace check share the same trigger condition, and the controller orders simultaneous triggers — either order is available to you, so this only helps.

### 5 blue-sentinel-trinity-force-hold
Mind/Body is a single-legend pair (VEN-149 Defender of Tomorrow, whose own abilities are gear-ready effects — irrelevant here). Turn A costs 4E+1P + 4E + 1 Body Power = 8E+2P.

### 6 brambleback-trinity-skyfall-conquer
R4 is load-bearing here and only here: the three Trinity Force triggers are appended Effect Text (434.1.c), and Skyfall says "**my** hold effects". Plain reading: text appended to the unit's Rules Text is the unit's. Counter-reading: "my" could be read as the unit's *printed* text only — that would drop this line to 1 point on the Conquer and is the reason it must be ruled on. Unit needs to be ready to Standard-Move (Accelerate 1E+Fury or wait a turn).

### 7 svellsongur-copy-hold
Two open readings. R5 (does Svellsongur work at all): its rules text is a Passive that "applies during the process of Attaching" — 725.1 names exactly that case, so 718.2 does not silence it; the copy lands in the Effect Text, which 718.3 keeps active. I rate R5 favourable. R6 (printed vs current text): "copy that unit's text" at attach time — the unit's Rules Text already includes earlier appended Effect Text (434.1.c), so the natural reading is *current*, which raises this entry to 11 (Sentinel: printed + copy + copy-of-(printed+copy) = 4 multipliers × 2 Ahri point sources + 1). The entry's 9 is the floor.

### 8 skyfall-ahri-conquer
Skyfall on Ahri's own printed trigger needs only the weak form of R4. Setup is heavy (≈35E+8P over several turns) and all five bodies must be at the target battlefield.

### 9–10 svellsongur-ornn-hold, guardian-passage-svellsongur
Only R5. Ornn's copy also carries "When you play me", which never fires again (the unit is already on the board). Guardian's return targets "a unit or gear" — Svellsongur itself if it ever dies.

### 11 world-atlas-sentinel-gold
Clean. Gold (187.5) is a gear token: it is fodder for Bottled Constellation and, because it readies for your next Beginning Phase, the only realistic Power source for Power Nexus (§22).

### 12 gauntlets-enforcer-conquer
Corpus: "[Equip] 3E + rainbow. This ability's **Energy** cost is reduced by the Might of the unit you choose." The Power is never reduced. Both triggers share one condition and each fires once per Conquer; nothing multiplies. Keep only as a Fury/Order placeholder.

### 13 azir-aphelios-attach
Per attach you pay the Equip cost (Eye: 1 Order Power = one rune recycled). Sequence 1 (Ready 2) → Weaponmaster to a Sand Soldier for 1 less (free) → re-Equip to Aphelios (1 Power, Channel 1) is −1E −2 runes recycled +2 ready +1 channelled: about even. The legend makes one Soldier per turn (exhaust), so the third mode needs another Equipment played from hand. Correctly capped by "hasn't been chosen this turn".

### 14 ashe-retreat-hand-strip
Ashe costs 5E + 1 Order Power every cast; the entry's `needs` should include infinite-power (verified line exists). Retreat's channel goes to *you* (Ashe's owner). "When they hold, return it" — if you hold both battlefields they never Hold, so the strip is permanent. R7 applies to Retreat.

### 15 promising-future-force-deck
With your deck empty you look at 0 and play nothing (431.1.c, no Burn Out) — fine. The opponent chooses which of their top 5 to play and may pick a spell that resolves against you or a unit that blocks you; they still need Power for it and the text does not say what happens if they cannot pay. Their deck loses 1 card per cast and the other 4 recycle to the bottom, so decking them takes ~40 casts and 431.2.b refills their deck from trash before you gain the Burn Out point. The article's use is as a one-off; as a repeated engine it is net negative.

### 16 ruination-sprite-burst-clear
Two ready 3-Might Sprites per Sprite Burst can Standard-Move base → battlefield the same turn and Establish Control uncontested after The Ruination: +2 Conquer points (470) on the casting turn. They die at the start of your next Beginning Phase before scoring, so they never Hold — put non-Temporary bodies there for the Holds (§18 does this with Soaring Scouts).

### 17 bloodharbor-bewitching-discard
Legend: "Return a friendly unit **at a battlefield** to its owner's hand" — the Spirit must be at a battlefield, so either play it to one you control or move it there the following turn. One discard per turn, bounded by the legend's exhaust; text and arithmetic exact.

### 18 time-warp-hold-burst
Full sequence from 0: Ruination; Sprite Burst; Sprites conquer both battlefields (+2); Soaring Scouts played to both; Time Warp (10E+4 Mind Power). Extra turn: Temporary kills the Sprites, Scouts Hold both (+2, now 4); Draw Phase draws the one card you left in the deck via Forge's spare slot; loop; Time Warp. Repeat: 6, 8. Hold points are not Conquer points, so 471.1.b never applies. Time Warp is banished (427), so the three copies must already be in hand — with Renata's Draw 1 you can draw the whole deck first.

### 19 renata-bubble-bot-ready
Hexplate's "[Effect] I am a Mech" is granted text (718.3), so Bubble Bot's "another friendly Mech" is a legal choice — solid. The R7 shortfall is self-healing here because Renata's own "1E + Mind: Draw 1" is usable (she is at a battlefield).

### 20 swain-double-conquer
Step 1 works (4 points, clause satisfied by Forge/Ekko/Shadow's Call). Step 2 fails: a replayed Swain enters exhausted (143.4) and Standard Move requires a ready unit. Repair, all inside Mind/Order and the loop: Retreat each Swain (1E), replay at base (6E+1P), then play Jayce (6E+1P) → ready one Swain; Mesmerize/Retreat Jayce and replay twice more → three ready Swains; Standard-Move to B; Conquer +4. Each Jayce cycle also re-arms his gear clause (new object, 124), so the loop's next Forge play readies a second Swain per cycle — two Jayce cycles suffice. This turns the entry into a 5-card line and must be re-authored, not promoted.

### 21 bottled-constellation-time-warp
Fodder: the loop's Recruits (1/pass) and any Gold. Constellation points are not Conquer points (471.1.a.1). Same in-hand Time Warp and non-empty-deck cautions as §18.

### 22 power-nexus-sentinel
R8: the Nexus trigger is yours by 190.6 and is worded "When you hold here", so calling it one of "your hold effects for holding here" is the plain reading; counter-reading (Sentinel only multiplies *unit* abilities) is weak. The real wound is Power: 4 Power per point, paid in the Beginning Phase when the Rune Pool is empty (167) — 12 runes recycled for 3 points leaves you with nothing to channel back but 2/turn. Gold from World Atlas (§11) is the fix; note it in the entry instead of "12 Power".

### 23 ivern-sentinel-hold
Ivern's trigger is "When I conquer **or** hold", so it is a hold effect for Sentinel. The Reckoner's Arena question the note raises is correctly left out.

### 24 kharox-sanction-burn
Corpus: mode 2 is "Disempower a unit **that's [Empowered]**", so two consecutive mode-2 Sanctions on Kharox are impossible; the entry's steps need one mode-1 cast in the middle. 828.1.d confirms the crossing trigger. Kharox's own Empower ability ("Use only if not Empowered") is re-usable after each Disempower for 6E+2 Chaos, so the per-turn cap is Energy, not Sanction copies.

### 25 grand-plaza-loop-time-warp
Forge's Recruit is played "at your base"; with Industrialist it is ready and walks to the Plaza (144.4.a). Twelve passes give twelve bodies. The opponent's units at the Plaza are handled by The Ruination first. Clean.

### 26 zed-clone-eye-recruits
R9: 420.1 defines Moving as any Game Object moving between Locations, which is the reading Riot's Stealthy Pursuer ban statement relied on; Zed's swap is a Move. Zed must have conquered once to own a Clone (his trigger plays it "to your base"); Eye on the Clone costs 1 Order Power. Rune arithmetic corrected upward in the table. Zed ends at the Plaza after an even number of swaps.

### 27 leblanc-temporary-plaza
R10: Temporary "is a Triggered Ability keyword" (816.1) — LeBlanc's "Your [Temporary] effects at my battlefield don't trigger" suppresses it on the plain reading. Keeper must be played to the Plaza ("play two Reflection unit tokens **here**"), so the Plaza must be yours first (LeBlanc conquers it, or anything does). Reflection copies (187.6) become 1-Might Temporary Keepers without re-firing the play trigger.

### 28 jayce-mesmerize-renata
Jayce readies "something besides me that's exhausted" — Renata, anywhere. Two readies per Jayce object (on play, and on the first non-token gear that turn = the loop's Forge replay) halve the cost per point. Mesmerize needs Mind Power; Retreat does the same job for 1E.

## Shared readings a human must rule on (each once)

- **R1 — Blue Sentinel / Red Brambleback stack trigger instances on the single Score event.** 471.2.c says score abilities "cannot be triggered more than once per turn for a player". The cards say each hold/conquer effect "trigger[s] an additional time". Strongest counter-reading: 471.2.c is an absolute cap and the cards are blanks. That would make two printed cards do nothing, which 002 (card text supersedes) forbids; the entries' own reading (still one Score, more instances) needs neither 002 nor a second Score. Verdict: arguable but favourable. Decides entries 1, 5, 6, 7, 8, 11, 22, 23.
- **R2 — Card-text "score 1 point" is a point Gain, not a 469 Score,** so 470's once-per-battlefield cap does not apply and 471.1.a.1 exempts it from the Final Point rule. Already accepted implicitly by the verified Renata line (eight "Score 1 point" activations in a turn). Decides the point totals of every BURST entry.
- **R4 — Skyfall of Areion's "my hold effects" includes hold triggers granted by other Equipment** (434.1.c appends them to the bearer's Rules Text). Load-bearing for 6 only; 8 uses Ahri's printed trigger.
- **R5 — Svellsongur's copy is processed as it attaches (725.1) despite 718.2** and lives in its Effect Text afterwards. Decides 7, 9, 10.
- **R6 — Svellsongur copies current (appended) text, not printed.** Only moves 7 from 9 to 11; harmless to 9 and 10.
- **R7 — Loop draw budget.** The verified Lux engine recycles 4 cards and draws exactly 4 per pass; Forge's "spare slot" is a *recycle* slot, not a draw. Every extra card that must return through the deck (Retreat, Mesmerize, Promising Future, Sprite Burst) needs one extra draw per pass — Renata Mastermind's verified "1E + Mind: Draw 1" supplies it at 1E+1P — or three copies held in hand. Time Warp cannot ride the loop at all (banished). Affects 14, 15, 16, 18, 19, 20, 21, 25, 28; none of them say so.
- **R8 — A battlefield's own "When you hold here" ability is one of "your hold effects" (190.6).** Decides 22.
- **R9 — "When I move" fires on effect moves (420.1), and "here" is the post-move location.** Decides 26 (same reading as the verified Pursuer/Herald entry and Riot's ban statement).
- **R10 — LeBlanc's "Temporary effects don't trigger" suppresses the 816.1.b kill trigger.** Decides 27.
- **R11 — Units enter exhausted (143.4) unless card text overrides it** (Industrialist for tokens, Accelerate). Kills 20 as written; the Plaza lines 25/26 depend on Industrialist for same-turn movement.
