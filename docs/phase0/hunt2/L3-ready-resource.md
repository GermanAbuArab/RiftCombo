## Target (a): net-positive Energy or Power engine outside Mind+Order

What the read shows (evidence, not a structural claim): the only "ready ALL runes" text is Ekko (Mind); the only cheap draw-4 plus recycle-4 package is Shadow's Call / Sacrifice / Forge (Order); every other domain pair's recursion (Fizz, Annie Stubborn, Heedless, Guardian of the Passage) costs Power that only Malzahar (Mind, once per ready) or Jhin (per move) refunds, and Jhin's readies are all card- or turn-bounded (L3-01/02/03). Verdict: strong bounded ENGINEs exist in Fury/Body, Fury/Calm and Mind/Body; nothing unbounded surfaced. The catalogue's "only verified infinite resource line is Lux" stands on this read.
