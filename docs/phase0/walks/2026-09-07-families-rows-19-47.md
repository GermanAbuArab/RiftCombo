# Matrix families, rows 19–47 — second pass

Issue [#168](https://github.com/GermanAbuArab/RiftCombo/issues/168). Session `rc-walk-fam2`, 2026-09-07.

## Method

The two-card CAUSE/TRIGGER matrix built on 2026-09-06 (`.scratch/cands.json`; scripts `.scratch/matrix.ts`,
`buckets.mjs`, `top30b.mjs`; method in `2026-09-06-finisher-feeders.md` §16) holds 1,522 unrefused
same-identity pairs in 47 trigger groups. The first pass walked ONE entry per trigger card with its best
partner. The bar for this pass is higher: **every partner whose numbers or trap differ from every existing
entry on that trigger card is its own entry**; a partner with the same mechanism and the same arithmetic is
a `notable` on the existing entry, not a new row.

Slice: rows 19–47 (friendly-moves, play-token-unit, attach-equip, friendly-dies ×7, buff ×3,
empower-other ×6, ready-friendly ×4, gear-ability, draw-second, become-mighty ×3).

Card text is verbatim from `data/corpus_flat.txt`. Every rules paragraph cited was opened in
`data/Riftbound-Core-Rules-2026-07-16.txt` and is quoted where it is load-bearing. Legend lists in
`prerequisites.easy` are built from `data/cards.json` by `.scratch/legends.mjs`, never typed from memory.

Refuse-by-rule buckets carried in from the first pass: **A** Hold payoff + Main-Phase engine (315 before
316, 167); **B** excess-damage + bloodless removal (465.1, R28 = A); **C** [Hidden] on the attacking side
(811.1.b, 811.1.d.2); **D** banned in every format (`data/legality.json`, whose `entries[].bases` is an
ARRAY); **E** a ready that buys nothing (315.1.b has already readied everything by the time a Hold fires;
144.1.b forbids the Standard Move in a Closed State).

## 1. friendly-dies (rows 23–29)

Seven trigger cards read the same event and pay seven different currencies: Viktor, Leader a Recruit token;
Wraith of Echoes a card (first each turn); Vanguard Helm a buff (only if the dying body carried one);
Altar of Memories a draw-and-filter (exhaust); Spectral Centaur +2 Might; Shard of Undoing a forced enemy
kill (Beginning Phase only); Vicious Snapjaws 1 XP.

### 1.1 A measured refusal: ten of the twenty-eight "causes" cannot fire any of them

The matrix's classifier for this event is a `/kill (a|an|…)/` shape, and it swept in every card that kills a
**gear**. A gear is not a unit, so none of these can ever fire a "when a friendly **unit** dies" trigger:

`OGN-056` Adaptatron, `OGN-224` Salvage, `SFD-005` Detonate, `SFD-032` Disarming Rake, `SFD-074` Pickpocket,
`SFD-077` Rocket Barrage (its "Kill a gear" mode), `SFD-084` Jayce, Man of Progress, `SFD-160` Zaun Punk,
`VEN-003` Brittle Steel, `VEN-080` Noxian Demolitionist — **10 of 28**.

This is the same class of classifier trap as `/kill (a|an|…)/` matching "kill **al**l gear", recorded by the
first pass. It is worth counting rather than dropping: it is why the group's "27 partners" is really ~17.

### 1.3 The rules the batch turned on, quoted

- **383.3.b** — *"If a Triggered Ability contains a cost within instructions at the beginning of the effect
  or immediately following the “you may” or “they may” that appears as the first part of the effect, that
  cost is treated as the base cost of the Triggered Ability."* with **383.3.b.1** *"The cost must be paid in
  order to finalize the Triggered Ability to the Chain."* So Altar of Memories must be READY at the instant
  the friendly unit dies; there is no window to ready it in response, and a second death the same turn finds
  it exhausted. Read against **415.3.a** — *"A player Readies all non-spell Game Objects they Control during
  the Awakening Phase on their turn"* — the cap is one activation **per player turn**, not per round trip.
- **355.10.e** — *"It is part of a set of objects chosen in whole or in part by other players. e.g., “Each
  player kills a unit they control” does not target."* The worked example is Safety Inspector's and Shard of
  Undoing's own wording. Consequence: **809.1.c / 809.1.d** never charge their [Deflect] tax, and the
  opponent picks their worst body. Both entries price this as attrition, not as aimed removal.
- **383.3.d** — *"If more than one Triggered Ability is Triggered simultaneously, then the player that
  controls the Abilities selects the order to place them on the Chain."* Three of your triggers fire at the
  start of your Beginning Phase in the Sprite Queen line (the Lab's kill, the standing Sprite's [Temporary],
  Sprite Queen's new Sprite); ordering the Lab first is what turns a death you were taking anyway into a card.
- **383.3.e.1** — *"Such a Triggered Ability will only be performed the specified number of times each
  turn."* Shard of Undoing's "the first time … each turn" is one execution, so the Lab route and the
  [Temporary] route are **alternatives**, not a doubling. The Lab is worth a card, never a second kill.
- **816.1.c** — *"The Trigger Condition is the controller of the permanent's Beginning Phase starting."* A
  Sprite born at the start of Beginning Phase N survives turn N and dies at the start of N+1, which is what
  makes the schedule a steady state.
- **323.6** — *"Players lose control of any controlled Battlefields without their Units occupying them if
  the turn is in an Open State and there is no Showdown or Combat ongoing there."* Reached here with a
  shorter chain than in `dusk-rose-lab-leblanc-beginning-draw`, because a Sprite has no Deathknell: the death
  makes a Cleanup Outstanding (**319.6**), the Shard's own trigger holds the state Neutral Closed (**310.2**)
  so 323.6 is false at that Cleanup; the Shard resolves, **319.5** makes another, and that one is Neutral
  Open (**310.1**) and strips the Lab — still before **315.2.b.2** Holds. **A Sprite is never a garrison.**
- **702.3 / 702.3.a** against a plain modifier: Spectral Centaur's "+2 :rb_might: this turn" and Deathgrip's
  "+Might equal to its Might" are continuous modifiers, not Buff counters, so the one-buff-per-unit cap never
  touches them and they stack. Same distinction the project drew for Lee Sin, Centered and Soul Shepherd.
- **194.1.c** with **471.1.a.1** (*"points Gained from sources that are not Conquer are not beholden to
  these restrictions"*) and R2 = A: Bottled Constellation's "score 1 point" is a Gain, so 470's
  once-per-battlefield cap does not touch it and the eighth point can come from there.
- **185.2.b / 185.2.d** make a Recruit token a unit for the Constellation's count; **186.1** keeps it out of
  the trash, so nothing reanimates or recycles it (**185**, **416.1**).
