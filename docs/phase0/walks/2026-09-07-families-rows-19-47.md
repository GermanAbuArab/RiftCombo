# Matrix families, rows 19–47 — second pass

Issue [#168](https://github.com/GermanAbuArab/RiftCombo/issues/168). Session `rc-walk-fam2`, 2026-09-07.

## Method

The two-card CAUSE/TRIGGER matrix built on 2026-09-06 (`.scratch/cands.json`; scripts `.scratch/matrix.ts`,
`buckets.mjs`, `top30b.mjs`; method in `2026-09-06-finisher-feeders.md` §16) holds 1,522 unrefused
same-identity pairs in 47 trigger groups. The first pass walked ONE entry per trigger card with its best
partner. The bar for this pass is higher: **every partner whose numbers or trap differ from every existing
entry on that trigger card is its own entry**; a partner with the same mechanism and the same arithmetic is
a `notable` on the existing entry, not a new row.

Slice: rows 19–47 (friendly-moves, play-token-unit, attach-equip, friendly-dies ×7, buff ×3,
empower-other ×6, ready-friendly ×4, gear-ability, draw-second, become-mighty ×3).

Card text is verbatim from `data/corpus_flat.txt`. Every rules paragraph cited was opened in
`data/Riftbound-Core-Rules-2026-07-16.txt` and is quoted where it is load-bearing. Legend lists in
`prerequisites.easy` are built from `data/cards.json` by `.scratch/legends.mjs`, never typed from memory.

Refuse-by-rule buckets carried in from the first pass: **A** Hold payoff + Main-Phase engine (315 before
316, 167); **B** excess-damage + bloodless removal (465.1, R28 = A); **C** [Hidden] on the attacking side
(811.1.b, 811.1.d.2); **D** banned in every format (`data/legality.json`, whose `entries[].bases` is an
ARRAY); **E** a ready that buys nothing (315.1.b has already readied everything by the time a Hold fires;
144.1.b forbids the Standard Move in a Closed State).

## 1. friendly-dies (rows 23–29)

Seven trigger cards read the same event and pay seven different currencies: Viktor, Leader a Recruit token;
Wraith of Echoes a card (first each turn); Vanguard Helm a buff (only if the dying body carried one);
Altar of Memories a draw-and-filter (exhaust); Spectral Centaur +2 Might; Shard of Undoing a forced enemy
kill (Beginning Phase only); Vicious Snapjaws 1 XP.

### 1.1 A measured refusal: ten of the twenty-eight "causes" cannot fire any of them

The matrix's classifier for this event is a `/kill (a|an|…)/` shape, and it swept in every card that kills a
**gear**. A gear is not a unit, so none of these can ever fire a "when a friendly **unit** dies" trigger:

`OGN-056` Adaptatron, `OGN-224` Salvage, `SFD-005` Detonate, `SFD-032` Disarming Rake, `SFD-074` Pickpocket,
`SFD-077` Rocket Barrage (its "Kill a gear" mode), `SFD-084` Jayce, Man of Progress, `SFD-160` Zaun Punk,
`VEN-003` Brittle Steel, `VEN-080` Noxian Demolitionist — **10 of 28**.

This is the same class of classifier trap as `/kill (a|an|…)/` matching "kill **al**l gear", recorded by the
first pass. It is worth counting rather than dropping: it is why the group's "27 partners" is really ~17.

### 1.3 The rules the batch turned on, quoted

- **383.3.b** — *"If a Triggered Ability contains a cost within instructions at the beginning of the effect
  or immediately following the “you may” or “they may” that appears as the first part of the effect, that
  cost is treated as the base cost of the Triggered Ability."* with **383.3.b.1** *"The cost must be paid in
  order to finalize the Triggered Ability to the Chain."* So Altar of Memories must be READY at the instant
  the friendly unit dies; there is no window to ready it in response, and a second death the same turn finds
  it exhausted. Read against **415.3.a** — *"A player Readies all non-spell Game Objects they Control during
  the Awakening Phase on their turn"* — the cap is one activation **per player turn**, not per round trip.
- **355.10.e** — *"It is part of a set of objects chosen in whole or in part by other players. e.g., “Each
  player kills a unit they control” does not target."* The worked example is Safety Inspector's and Shard of
  Undoing's own wording. Consequence: **809.1.c / 809.1.d** never charge their [Deflect] tax, and the
  opponent picks their worst body. Both entries price this as attrition, not as aimed removal.
- **383.3.d** — *"If more than one Triggered Ability is Triggered simultaneously, then the player that
  controls the Abilities selects the order to place them on the Chain."* Three of your triggers fire at the
  start of your Beginning Phase in the Sprite Queen line (the Lab's kill, the standing Sprite's [Temporary],
  Sprite Queen's new Sprite); ordering the Lab first is what turns a death you were taking anyway into a card.
- **383.3.e.1** — *"Such a Triggered Ability will only be performed the specified number of times each
  turn."* Shard of Undoing's "the first time … each turn" is one execution, so the Lab route and the
  [Temporary] route are **alternatives**, not a doubling. The Lab is worth a card, never a second kill.
- **816.1.c** — *"The Trigger Condition is the controller of the permanent's Beginning Phase starting."* A
  Sprite born at the start of Beginning Phase N survives turn N and dies at the start of N+1, which is what
  makes the schedule a steady state.
- **323.6** — *"Players lose control of any controlled Battlefields without their Units occupying them if
  the turn is in an Open State and there is no Showdown or Combat ongoing there."* Reached here with a
  shorter chain than in `dusk-rose-lab-leblanc-beginning-draw`, because a Sprite has no Deathknell: the death
  makes a Cleanup Outstanding (**319.6**), the Shard's own trigger holds the state Neutral Closed (**310.2**)
  so 323.6 is false at that Cleanup; the Shard resolves, **319.5** makes another, and that one is Neutral
  Open (**310.1**) and strips the Lab — still before **315.2.b.2** Holds. **A Sprite is never a garrison.**
- **702.3 / 702.3.a** against a plain modifier: Spectral Centaur's "+2 :rb_might: this turn" and Deathgrip's
  "+Might equal to its Might" are continuous modifiers, not Buff counters, so the one-buff-per-unit cap never
  touches them and they stack. Same distinction the project drew for Lee Sin, Centered and Soul Shepherd.
- **194.1.c** with **471.1.a.1** (*"points Gained from sources that are not Conquer are not beholden to
  these restrictions"*) and R2 = A: Bottled Constellation's "score 1 point" is a Gain, so 470's
  once-per-battlefield cap does not touch it and the eighth point can come from there.
- **185.2.b / 185.2.d** make a Recruit token a unit for the Constellation's count; **186.1** keeps it out of
  the trash, so nothing reanimates or recycles it (**185**, **416.1**).

## 2. attach-equip (rows 21–22), play-token-unit (row 20), friendly-moves (row 19), buff (row 32)

### 2.1 The rules fact this batch turned on: a re-attach to the same host is a no-op

**434.1.g** — *"Attaching a card to its current Top-Most Card will not have any effect."*
**434.1.h** — *"If a Game Effect instructs a player to Attach a card to its current Top-Most Card, nothing
additional happens."*
**434.1.f** — *"Attaching a card to a new Top-Most Card will cause it to Detach from the card to which it is
currently Attached."*

So every *"when you attach an Equipment to me"* payoff — Aphelios, Exalted and Jax, Unrelenting are the pool's
two — needs a **second host to shuttle the Equipment through**. You cannot pay an [Equip] cost twice on the
same unit for two triggers. This was not stated anywhere in the project and it prices the whole family:
one free attach source plus one paid [Equip] is **one trigger a turn**, not two.

`SFD-208 Forge of the Fluft` (colourless battlefield, *"While you control this battlefield, friendly legends
have ':rb_exhaust:: Attach an Equipment you control to a unit you control.'"*) was **in no entry before this
pass** and is the first repeatable attach source in the catalogue that costs no card. It is not the
[Weaponmaster] discount: 818.1 makes Equip an Activated Ability and [Weaponmaster] equips *"to me"* on the
play only, i.e. once per body played; the Forge is once per TURN, to any unit, forever. Its price is
**Control** of the battlefield — 190.1, *"Control is established over Battlefields through the course of
play"* — and the legend's only exhaust.

## 3. ready-friendly (rows 39–42), gear-ability (43), draw-second (44), become-mighty (45–47)

### 3.1 The result that collapses rows 45–47, stated as arithmetic

`SFD-205 Grand Duelist` and `OGN-249 Relentless Storm` both read *"you may **exhaust me** to channel 1 rune
exhausted"*. The exhaust is the cap: **playing or crossing three Mighty bodies in one turn still channels one
rune.** So of the 296 pairs the matrix put in these three rows, almost every one is a `notable` on an
existing entry, not a new row — the partner does not change the number.

The one thing that DOES change the number is **415.3.a** — *"A player Readies all non-spell Game Objects they
Control during the Awakening Phase on their turn"* — so an exhaust spent on the **opponent's** turn is
refunded before your own. Two entries were written on that, one from each side, and they state why the two
legends want opposite decks:

- **`grand-duelist-fiora-victorious-off-turn-crossing`.** 709's event, reached at Reaction speed. The
  finding: **a "+N Might this turn" is a REPEATABLE Mighty crossing and a buff is a one-time one.** 703 makes
  a Buff exactly +1 and 702.3 stops a second, so a buffed Might-4 body crosses once, ever — the ceiling
  `grand-duelist-arena-bar-mighty-channel` runs into. 317.2.c expires a "this turn" pump, so
  `OGN-232 Fiora, Victorious` (Might 4, *"While I'm [Mighty], I have [Deflect], [Ganking], and [Shield]"*, in
  no entry before) is back under the line every Cleanup and crosses again next turn.
- **`relentless-storm-ambush-mighty-off-turn-channel`.** The Storm reads *"when you **play** a [Mighty]
  unit"*, so no crossing is involved and 709 never applies. **822.1.b** — *"[Ambush] … is functionally short
  for 'I may be played to a battlefield where you control Units' and 'I have [Reaction] as long as I'm being
  played to a battlefield where you control Units.'"* — is how a Mighty body gets played on the opponent's
  turn. `UNL-120 Rengar, Trophy Hunter` (M6, and the only [Ambush] body whose own text lifts the
  friendly-garrison requirement) and `UNL-012 Lord Broadmane` (M5) were both in no entry.

### 3.2 Row 44 reopened: Frigid Jewel is not a regex artifact, its 100 partners are

`UNL-074 Frigid Jewel` reads *"When you draw your **second** card each turn"*. The first pass refused the row
because one extra draw arms it and every further draw is wasted — right about the partners, wrong about the
card. The correct partner is a single free draw that lands **before** the Draw Phase, and 315 gives the order:
315.1 Awaken, **315.2 Beginning Phase**, 315.3 Channel, **315.4 Draw Phase**. `OGN-101 Mushroom Pouch`
(*"At the start of your Beginning Phase, if you control a facedown card at a battlefield, draw 1"*) is the
only card in the pool that draws in 315.2, so the card the game hands you in 315.4.b is the second one and
the Jewel fires **every turn on a draw you were taking anyway**, for nothing → `frigid-jewel-mushroom-pouch-second-card`.
It never fires on the opponent's turn (you draw nothing in their Draw Phase), and the entry says so.

### 3.3 Row 43: [Equip] is a gear's activated ability, and it has no exhaust

**818.1** — *"Equip is an Activated Ability keyword"*; **818.1.a** *"present on Gear with the tag
Equipment"*; **818.1.b** *"Equip has a cost to activate and Attaches the card with Equip to a chosen Unit
when the cost is paid."* So every [Equip] payment is what `SFD-075 Prize of Progress` watches, and the
cheapest one in Mind — `SFD-073 Experimental Hexplate`, one Mind rune, no exhaust — can be paid as many times
a turn as you have Power, shuttling between two hosts because of **434.1.g** again.
→ `prize-of-progress-hexplate-equip-shuttle`, bounded by the Rune Deck (161.2.a's twelve, 315.3.b's two a
turn) rather than by how many gears the deck runs, which is what `prize-of-progress-seal-gear-might` is
bounded by.

### 3.4 Row 40: the Awaken's choice is made blind, and one of Jayce's three options has to be

`VEN-088 Jayce, Hammer in Hand` chooses [Assault 2], [Deflect 2] or [Ganking] whenever he becomes ready.
`awaken-ready-body-triple-trigger` takes that for free at 315.1.b — before the opponent has done anything.
`OGN-146 Wallop` is [Action], and **806.1.b** grants permission *"to be played or activated during Showdowns,
even when it is not the Controlling player's turn"*, so a second choice can be made with the board resolved.
**But [Deflect 2] cannot be one of them**: **809.1.c** makes Deflect *"an additional cost"* on spells that
target him, and a spell already on the Chain has had its cost determined — granting Deflect in response taxes
nothing. → `jayce-hammer-wallop-showdown-keyword`, which says which option belongs to which window.
**415.1.b / 415.1.c** are the gate: a ready aimed at a ready Jayce is thrown away and the trigger never fires.

## 4. Second partners on rows 24–31 and 38–39, and the refusal that closes row 29

### 4.1 The same event, and 383.1.b collapses one card's trigger and not the other's

**383.1.b** — *"If an ability triggers “the [Nth] time” something happens and that trigger condition is met
multiple times simultaneously, the ability's controller picks one of those instances to serve as the trigger
condition. The ability triggers only once, due to the chosen condition."* — and **its worked example is
`OGN-118 Wraith of Echoes` by name**. So the Wraith draws ONE card however many bodies die at once, and the
right partner for him is the one that extracts the most from a **single** death:
`OGN-208 Cruel Patron` (Might 6 for 4 Energy and no Power, in no entry before) →
`wraith-of-echoes-cruel-patron-sacrifice-body`.

`UNL-068 Spectral Centaur` reads *"When another friendly unit dies"* with no Nth-time wording, so 383.1.b does
not apply and **383.3.d** does: three simultaneous deaths are three triggers. `VEN-067 Bottled Constellation`
kills three at once, so the Centaur is +6 and you gain a point →
`spectral-centaur-bottled-constellation-triple-death`. The two entries are written as a deliberate pair.

A registered wording detail: the printed text in `data/corpus_flat.txt` is *"The first time **a** friendly
unit dies each turn"* — no *"another"* — while the Core Rules example paraphrases it with one. Rule 002 makes
the printed text govern, so **the Wraith's own death pays for a card.** Recorded here, not in
`data/errata.json`, which is reserved for dated Riot errata.

### 4.2 A uniqueness claim, swept rather than assumed

`grep -inE "dies.*buff|buff.*dies"` over the whole corpus returns **exactly one card**: `OGN-228 Vanguard
Helm`. It is the only card in the pool that puts a buff back into circulation when its host dies, which is
what makes it worth a slot — 702.3 / 702.3.a make a buff a one-per-body placement and 703 makes it exactly
+1, so buffs are a scarce resource, not a repeatable pump. → `vanguard-helm-kinkou-monk-buff-conservation`
(`OGN-141 Kinkou Monk`, two placements a card, in no entry before). The entry names the legend that switches
it off: `OGN-269 The Boss` is a legal Body/Order legend whose would-die replacement means **no death event
occurs**, so the Helm never triggers — the 808.1.d.1 shape applied to a buff.

### 4.3 Row 29 REFUSED with a complete sweep: every XP sink in the pool is bounded

`UNL-129 Vicious Snapjaws` (*"When another friendly unit dies, gain 1 XP"*) is the pool's only death-to-XP
faucet and is in no entry. It stays that way, because the sinks do not exist. Sweeping
`grep -nE "Spend [0-9]+ XP"` over the whole corpus returns **7 cards and 8 costs**:

| card | cost | what bounds it |
|---|---|---|
| `UNL-109` Blood Rose | Spend 3 XP, **exhaust**: Ready a unit | its own exhaust, one a turn |
| `UNL-201` Voidreaver | Spend 1 XP, **exhaust**: Buff a unit / Spend 2 XP, **exhaust**: Move | its own exhaust, one a turn |
| `UNL-203` Keeper of the Hammer | Spend 3 XP, **exhaust**: Draw 1 | its own exhaust, one a turn |
| `UNL-158` Shepherd's Heirloom | [Equip] — Spend 1 XP | 434.1.g: a re-attach to the same host is a no-op |
| `UNL-102` Crowd Favorite | Spend 2 XP: Buff me | 702.3 — one buff per unit, so once ever |
| `UNL-162` Enthralling Protector | Spend 2 XP: Buff me | 702.3 — same |
| `UNL-126` Megatusk | Spend 3 XP: Give your units **here** [Ganking] this turn | 810.1.c.3: [Ganking] is a permission, so a second grant at the same battlefield does nothing; a Duel has two battlefields (485.4), so the honest ceiling is two activations, six XP, per turn |

So **five sinks are capped by an exhaust, two by 702.3, and the last by the fact that its effect is a
permission** — no XP faucet in the pool can outrun its outlets, which confirms and sharpens the project's
standing note that XP sinks are a bounded step function. Chaos, Snapjaws' own domain, prints **no [Level]
static at all**, so his XP has nowhere to go inside identity; outside it, Body/Chaos reaches only Voidreaver,
which is exhaust-gated at one activation a turn. Refused.

### 4.4 The rule that bounds rows 33–38, and the one anchor that had no entry

**441.1.b** — *"An Empowered Game Object can not be Empowered."* — with **441.1.c** (*"nothing additional
happens"*) and **441.2** (a permanent board state). So every *"When I become [Empowered]"* payoff fires ONCE
per copy per game unless something removes the state, and **the partner set of the whole family is the
disempowerers, not the empowerers.** Five of the six anchors in rows 33–38 already had entries;
`VEN-047 Apprentice Mage` — the cheapest [Empower] cost in the pool that is pure Energy (2, no Power, no
exhaust, no discard) — had none. → `apprentice-mage-sanction-empower-reset`, whose honest product is deck
sculpting: **436.4.a** (*"The Player will not perform a Burn Out as a result of Predicting with too few cards
in their deck"*) makes [Predict 2] one of the few selection effects safe inside an empty-deck shell, and
**416.1** sends what it recycles to the bottom, so it never tutors.

### 4.5 Row 39: Irelia's trigger reads CHOOSE, which is wider than the catalogue had used

`SFD-057 Irelia, Fervent` is *"When you **choose or ready** me"*. **702.2.a** (*"To Buff a Unit, a player
chooses a Unit and then places a buff on it"*) and 355.10 make most interactions a choosing, and **809.1.c**
taxes only *"Spells and abilities AN OPPONENT CONTROLS"* — so every one of your own interactions with her is
a free +1. `blade-dancer-irelia-defiant-dance` reaches her through the READY half; `UNL-045 Forgotten
Signpost` reaches her through the CHOOSE half for nothing, every turn, at Showdown speed (806.1.b) →
`irelia-fervent-forgotten-signpost-choose`. One +1 per activation and never two: the Signpost chooses a payer
and a mover, but the effect says *"a **different** unit"*.

Two citations were corrected during this batch rather than shipped: **808.1.c** (*"Deathknell … is functionally

Two facts that carry beyond their rows:

- **144.4 is why the Fae Porter can never collect his own battlefield's departure rider.** The Standard Move
  is base↔battlefield; battlefield→battlefield needs [Ganking], and **810.1.c.3** says [Ganking] *"does not

  battlefield with a *"when a unit moves from here"* rider pays the bodies an effect drags OFF it, never the
  body that walked in from the base.
- **The Awaken is a phase EARLIER than the Beginning Phase (315.1 before 315.2), so a payoff whose cost is
  its own exhaust is free against any scheduled Beginning-Phase death.** That is the whole reason
  Altar of Memories works on a Dusk Rose Lab kill, and it generalises the project's existing note about
  [Temporary]-death payoffs.

### 5.2 Where the slice runs out

At the bar this pass set — *every partner whose numbers or trap differ from every existing entry on that
trigger card is its own entry* — rows 19–47 are now mined. Twenty-four entries, one row refused outright with
a complete sweep (29), one carried refusal honoured (47, Fiora Worthy: thirteen entries and two synergy rules
of her own), and rows 45–46 reduced from 296 candidate pairs to two entries by the exhaust arithmetic.

What is left is deliberately NOT written, and why:

- **Row 20, `UNL-160 Ultrasoft Poro` and `SFD-198 Arise!` under Lillia.** Both make token bodies that Lillia
  grants [Tank]; the Poro's Birds already print [Deflect], which is the same combination
  `lillia-flurry-of-feathers-tank-wall` has, and Arise!'s 2-Might Sand Soldiers change the wall's size and
  nothing else. Same mechanism, same arithmetic → notables on the existing rows.
- **Row 21, `SFD-056 Sterak's Gage` under Aphelios.** Its printed [Quick-Draw] is the two-card version of
  what `aphelios-jax-quickdraw-attach` already does through Jax, Unmatched. Same mechanism.
- **Row 30, `OGN-152 Mistfall` beside Fretful Feline.** Mistfall converts a buff into a ready, which is the
  same buff→ready conversion `simian-ancestor-arena-bar-wallop-ready` walks and the same Main-Phase-ready
  payoff `first-mate-fretful-feline-pirates-haven-main-phase-ready` prices, with 702.3 capping it identically.
- **Rows 33–38 beyond the Apprentice Mage.** The remaining disempowerers are `VEN-133 Glowstone` (it
  disempowers itself), `VEN-127 Lacerate` and `VEN-037 Tomb-Raider Barbara` (both aimed at ENEMY permanents,
  so neither resets your own payoff). The family has no uncovered reset left.
- **Row 19 beyond two entries.** `SFD-079 Bard, Mercurial` moves your whole board to an **open** battlefield,
  and 344.2's Showdown route assigns no damage at all — so the Bar's +1 Might per departure is worth exactly
  nothing on that line. Refused on the arithmetic, not on the reading.
