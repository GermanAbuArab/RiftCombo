# Hand walks — the five entries R6, R10 and R28 unblocked

**Date:** 2026-09-04 · **Rules version:** Core Rules 2026-07-16 · **All five HOLD.**

R6 = A, R10 = A and R28 = A were ruled on issue #11 this session. A ruling never promotes an entry by
itself, so each of the five is walked here against card text and rule numbers.

## The Final Point check, common to all three point lines

Worth doing once, because it is the rule that could have killed every BURST at the last step.
**471.1.b** — *"When a player tries to Gain a Point **through a Conquer**, and their current Point Total is
1 point from the Victory Score or higher … If the player has Scored every Battlefield this turn, that
player Gains the Final Point. If not, that player draws a card instead."* And **471.1.a.1** — *"points
Gained from sources that are **not Conquer** are not beholden to these restrictions."*

So the restriction reaches only Conquer Scores. Under **R2 = A** the card-text "score 1 point" of Ahri and
Tryndamere is a point Gain by ability (194.1.c), not a 469 Score, so none of the ability points are
beholden. And the ordering is safe anyway: **471.1** gains the Score's own point *before* **471.2**
triggers the Score abilities, so the single Conquer/Hold point is never the eighth.

## 1. `leblanc-temporary-plaza` — ten bodies that refuse to die — **HOLDS**

**UNL-090 LeBlanc, Everywhere at Once** (Unit, Mind, E4 M4): `[Backline]` ·
**"Your [Temporary] effects at my battlefield don't trigger."**
**UNL-081 Keeper of Masks** (Unit, Mind, E2 M1): `[Hidden]` · `[Temporary]` ·
**"When you play me, play two Reflection unit tokens here. Then do this: They become copies of me."**
**OGN-293 The Grand Plaza**: "When you hold here, if you have 7+ units here, you win the game."

- **R10 = A** switches off the kill: 816.1 makes `[Temporary]` a Triggered Ability keyword, 383 makes
  Triggered Abilities effects, and LeBlanc names the bracketed keyword. Nothing between the three.
- **The Keepers can be played at the Plaza.** This was the one thing that could have broken it, because
  `[Ambush]` (822.1.b/c) reads as though a battlefield were not a default play location. It is:
  **355.2.a** — *"By default, Valid locations include the controller's Base **or a Battlefield the
  controller controls**."* Ambush only adds battlefields you do **not** control. So all three Keepers go
  straight to the Plaza and their Reflections appear "here" = the Plaza.
- **No recursion.** UNL-T06 Reflection: *"I become a copy of something when played. **I don't get that
  card's play effects**."* The copies never re-fire "When you play me".
- **Count:** 3 Keepers + 6 Reflections + LeBlanc = **10 units**, against a bar of 7.
- **Hidden is capped at one.** 811.1.b allows one facedown per battlefield, so exactly one Keeper is free;
  the other two cost E2 each. Four Energy for nine bodies.

**Two constraints the entry did not state.** Might is **not** a copyable trait (477.1.b.1.a lists Name,
Super Type, Type, Tags, Cost, Domain, Rules Text), so the Reflections stay at the token's printed **0
Might** rather than becoming M1. They do not die of it — 142.4's own example is *"A unit has 0 [M]. In
order to have lethal damage marked on it, it must have at least 1 damage marked on it"* — but any single
point of damage removes one. And killing LeBlanc un-suppresses every `[Temporary]` at once. The line
wants the Plaza uncontested or a Time Warp.

## 2. `tryndamere-brambleback-conquer` — 9 points on one Conquer — **HOLDS**

**OGN-034 Tryndamere, Barbarian** (Fury, E7 P2 M8): **"When I conquer after an attack, if you assigned 5
or more excess damage to enemy units, you score 1 point."**
**UNL-029 Red Brambleback** (Fury, E4 P1 M4): `[Accelerate]` ·
**"Your conquer effects for conquering here trigger an additional time."**

- **R28 = A** gives the trigger a meaning: excess damage is attacking Might that never got assigned,
  because **465.2.c.4** forbids assigning a unit more damage than the minimum needed to kill it. The
  declared board attacks with 2×8 + 3×4 = **28 Might**, so any garrison under 23 total Might leaves the
  required 5 over. Not a real constraint.
- **Arithmetic against the entry's own `uses`,** which is what the 2026-09-04 BURST audit exists to force:
  T = 2 Tryndamere, K = 3 Brambleback, formula `1 + T × (1 + K)` = 1 + 2 × 4 = **9**. Clears 8 in one
  Conquer, so the BURST class is honest. (The earlier T=1, K=2 board its own REFUTE note had flagged gives
  4.)
- **R1 = A with stacking** is what makes each Brambleback add an instance; **R2 = A** is what keeps 470's
  once-per-battlefield cap off the eight ability points.
- **The attack is required.** "When I conquer **after an attack**" plus 383.4.e: the Attacker designation
  exists only inside a Combat, so an uncontested walk-in conquers but does not fire this. Defenders must
  be there — which is also what supplies the excess damage.

**Fragility the entry should carry:** all five bodies have to survive the combat to conquer, and the
Bramblebacks are M4 into whatever the garrison hits back with.

## 3. `svellsongur-copy-hold` — 11 points on one Hold — **HOLDS**

**SFD-059 Svellsongur** (Gear, Calm, E3 P1, `[Equip] 1 Energy + 1 Calm`): **"As this is attached to a
unit, copy that unit's text to this Equipment's effect text for as long as this is attached to it."**
**OGN-066 Ahri, Alluring** (Calm): "When I hold, you score 1 point."
**UNL-087 Blue Sentinel** (Mind): "Your hold effects for holding here trigger an additional time."

The existence chain was hand-checked on 2026-09-04 and stands: 718.2 deactivates only **printed** Rules
Text; 725.1 carves out exactly an attach-time passive; 136.2.b/136.2.c/718.3 keep the Effect Text active
and append it to the Top-Most Card; and 136.2.d makes "I" mean the unit, with Guardian Angel and
Brutalizer as the rules' own worked examples.

**R6 = A** settles the magnitude — the copy tracks current text, so the copies compound:

| | instances |
|---|---|
| Ahri, printed | 1 point source |
| + Svellsongur on Ahri | **N = 2** point sources |
| Blue Sentinel, printed | 1 extra trigger |
| + Svellsongur B on Sentinel (copies 1) | 2 extra triggers |
| + Svellsongur C on Sentinel (copies the **current** 2) | **K = 4** extra triggers |

`1 + N × (1 + K)` = 1 + 2 × 5 = **11**, against the 9 the snapshot reading would have given. Clears 8.
Under **R1 = A** the Sentinel adds trigger instances to the single Score rather than creating a second
one, so 471.2.c is not contradicted; under **R2 = A** the ability points escape 470.

## 4. `svellsongur-ornn-hold` and 5. `guardian-passage-svellsongur` — both **HOLD**

Neither needs R6 at all: one Svellsongur on one unit, so the conservative snapshot reading and the
current-text reading give the same number. Their only blocker was that nobody had walked 718.2 against
725.1 for Svellsongur, and that walk is now done (above).

- **SFD-058 Ornn, Blacksmith** (Calm): "When you play me **or when I hold**, look at the top 4 cards of
  your Main Deck. You may reveal a gear from among them and draw it. Then recycle the rest." The copy
  gives a second look-at-4 per Hold. The copied "when you play me" never re-fires — nothing plays the
  Equipment's effect text. **431.1.c** confirms looking never Burns Out, so a thin deck is safe; you just
  see fewer than 4.
- **SFD-035 Guardian of the Passage** (Calm, E6 M6): "When I hold, you may return a unit or gear from
  your trash to your hand." Two returns per Hold.

Both are two-card, single-domain (Calm) engines that fit gear decks carrying no other combo, which is
their real value in the catalogue.
