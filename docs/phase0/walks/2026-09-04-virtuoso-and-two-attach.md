# Hand walks — Virtuoso's banish set and the two Aphelios attach engines

**Date:** 2026-09-04 · **Rules version:** Core Rules 2026-07-16 · **All three HOLD.**

Three small ENGINEs, all honest about being small. What each walk had to confirm is that the *ceiling* the
entry names is the real one, because for engines the ceiling is the whole claim.

---

## 1. `virtuoso-banish-channel` — four big spells buy four ready runes — **HOLDS**

**UNL-181 Virtuoso** (Legend, Fury/Mind): **"When you play a spell, if you spent 4 or more Energy, you may
banish it. Then, if there are four spells banished with me, put each in its trash, channel 4 runes, and
draw 1."**

- **R26 = B**, ruled 2026-09-04, is what makes the legend work at all: the trigger waits until the spell
  has resolved and banishes it from the trash. Under the literal Chain reading Virtuoso would counter his
  own spell.
- **The runes are usable the turn they arrive.** 430.2.a — *"By default, runes are channeled readied."*
  The card says plain "channel 4 runes" with no "exhausted" rider, and 430.2 shows that a card wanting the
  other behaviour has to say so.
- **The ceiling is real and the entry states it correctly.** 161.2.a — *"Exactly 12 Rune cards chosen
  during Deck Construction."* So the four runes are **permanent ramp against a 12-card deck**, not +4
  Energy per cycle. Lifetime output is at most the runes you had not already channelled, plus one card per
  16+ Energy of spells. A deck channels 2 a turn for free anyway (315.3.b), so this is acceleration, not
  income.
- **The trigger completes on the fourth spell's own banish** — the ability banishes first, then checks
  "if there are four spells banished with me", inside one resolution.
- **"Spent 4+ Energy" is a spend check, not a printed-cost check**, so cost reduction can silently drop a
  4-cost spell under the line. The entry says this.

Legend-only, no second card. Its value in the catalogue is telling a Fury/Mind big-spell deck what the
payoff actually is and where it stops.

## 2. `azir-aphelios-attach` — three modes a turn, and no more — **HOLDS**

**SFD-247 Emperor of the Sands** (Legend, Calm/Order): "Sand Soldiers you play have `[Weaponmaster]`
(when they're played, you may `[Equip]` one of your Equipment to them for **1 rainbow less**). 1 Energy,
exhaust: Play a 2 Might Sand Soldier unit token to your base. **Use only if you've played an Equipment
this turn.**"
**SFD-049 Aphelios, Exalted** (Calm): **"When you attach an Equipment to me, choose one that hasn't been
chosen this turn — Ready 2 runes. / Channel 1 rune exhausted. / Buff a friendly unit."**

- **434.1.g/h is the rule the whole line turns on.** *"Attaching a card to its current Top-Most Card will
  not have any effect."* So an Equipment sitting on Aphelios cannot be re-attached to him for a second
  trigger — it has to leave first. The Sand Soldier is what it leaves onto, and Weaponmaster makes that
  hop free (Eye of the Herald's Equip is 1 Order rune, minus 1 rainbow).
- **"That hasn't been chosen this turn" is a hard cap of three**, and the legend exhausts, so it makes one
  Sand Soldier a turn. Two attaches to Aphelios come out of that loop; **the third mode needs a second
  Equipment played from hand**, exactly as the entry says.
- **Net per turn:** −1 Energy and 2 Order runes recycled for the Equip costs, against 2 runes readied and
  1 channelled. About even, plus a 2 Might body and a buff. ENGINE, correctly.

## 3. `aphelios-jax-quickdraw-attach` — the same three modes, paid for differently — **HOLDS**

**SFD-054 Jax, Unmatched** (Calm, E5 P1): "Your Equipment everywhere have `[Quick-Draw]`."
**SFD-193 Grandmaster at Arms** (Legend, Calm/Body): "1 Energy, **exhaust**: Attach a detached Equipment
you control to a unit you control. / **exhaust**: Attach an attached Equipment you control to a unit you
control."
**SFD-033 Doran's Shield** (Gear, Calm, E1, `[Equip] Calm`)

- **819.1.d** — Quick-Draw is *"functionally short for '[Reaction]' and 'When you play this, attach it to a
  Unit you control'."* So the attach rides the play and **no Equip Power is paid**, which is the entry's
  whole reason to exist next to the Azir version.
- **Both of Grandmaster's abilities exhaust her**, so there is exactly **one free re-attach a turn** — and
  by 434.1.g/h it has to move an Equipment **off another unit onto Aphelios**; moving one off him triggers
  nothing. The line therefore needs a second unit already carrying an Equipment every turn.
- **The arithmetic is net zero, and the entry says so.** Two 1-Energy Equipment out, two runes readied
  back, plus one exhausted rune channelled and one buff. Step 1 alone is +1 Energy; the full three-attach
  turn is not. (An exhausted channelled rune is still worth something — recycling a rune for Power carries
  no exhaust cost — but it is not Energy that turn.)
- **Jax is a 5E+1P tax** for the static, and Cloth Armor (SFD-064) is Mind, so it only serves the Calm/Mind
  variant, which has no Grandmaster at Arms and so no free re-attach. Both already in the notes.
