## 1. `tricksy-tentacles-yuumi-forced-defend-subset` — 355.11.b, a group target degrades to a SUBSET

**355.11.b, cited by nothing** (355.11 and 355.11.a both were):

> If the group of targets no longer collectively fulfill the targeting restriction as the spell or
> ability resolves, that spell or ability's controller can choose a subset of the original targets that
> fulfills the targeting requirement for the spell or ability to affect.

Riot's worked example is a pool card, `OGN-256 Fox-Fire`, and a defensive pump — and it ends by drawing
the boundary of the subset:

> They can't choose to affect units at the same battlefield that weren't initially chosen as targets.
> They can, however, choose to affect units that were initially chosen as targets that left the chosen
> battlefield before Fox-Fire resolved as long as those units are all located at the same battlefield.

So **a pump aimed at a total-Might restriction costs you one dragged body, never the spell**, and a
defensive move does not dodge it either as long as the survivors end up co-located. The pool prints
three group targets priced by summed Might (swept over `data/corpus_flat.txt`, 2026-09-09): `OGN-256
Fox-Fire`, `UNL-054 Tricksy Tentacles`, `VEN-107 Decree of Discord`. 355.12 is what makes each member a
target in the first place.

The line: Tricksy Tentacles drags up to 8 total Might of enemy bodies onto a battlefield **you** control,
so 450 and 190.3.a.1 apply Contested status and 464.2.c.1 makes **them** the Attacker on **your** turn;
464.2.e.1 places the Defender's triggers last and 340.1 resolves them first, so Yuumi's +3 Might and
[Tank] land before the damage step. 466.5 gives Control only to a player who did not already have it, so
the class is ENGINE and no point is scored.

## 2. `ki-barrier-shen-kinkou-prevent-pool` — 437.3, a Prevent Value is spent down and survives

**Fifteen sub-rules of the Prevent block were cited by nothing**: 437.1, .1.a, .1.a.1, .1.b, .1.b.1,
.1.b.1.a, .1.b.1.b, .1.b.2, .2, .2.a, .3, .3.a, .3.b, .3.c and 437.6. (437.4, 437.5, 437.5.a, 437.5.b
and 437.7 were already carried by `void-gate-ki-barrier-prevent-counts-bonus-damage`, rc-walk-fc / #191,
which this entry cross-references and does not duplicate.)

The obvious reading of `VEN-126 Ki Barrier` is that its Prevent 7 is a one-shot spent by whatever it
meets first. **It is not.** 437.2 replaces the damage with one reduced by the tracked Prevent Value;
437.3 reduces that value by the amount prevented; 437.3.b makes the reduced number the newly tracked
value; 437.3.a expires it only *"If the Prevent Value is 0 or less."* A Prevent 7 that eats a Deal 2 is
a Prevent 5 and keeps working — **a pool for the turn**.

That makes the pool's two bounded Prevents **opposite tools**, which no entry had said. 437.1.b.2:
*"Prevent will always apply to the next damage that would be dealt to a unit affected by the Prevent
action."* `SFD-194 Counter Strike` reads *"The next time that unit would be dealt damage this turn,
prevent it"* — one instance of **any size**, the answer to `OGS-022 Final Spark` (8, the pool's largest
single Deal) and spent entirely by a 1-damage ping. Ki Barrier is the answer to a spell with several
Deal actions: against `OGN-248 Icathian Rain`, which prints *"Deal 2 to a unit."* six times, it absorbs
2 + 2 + 2 and 1 of the fourth — **seven of twelve**, where Counter Strike absorbs two. Counter Strike is
also Signature (Jax), so 103.2.d.2 forces a legend on any list that runs it; Ki Barrier is not.

437.3.c — *"If the Prevent Value is 'All' then it remains 'All.'"* — is why the other two Prevent cards
are different in kind and why the 437.3 arithmetic is visible on exactly one card. Four Prevent rows in
the pool, swept 2026-09-09: `OGN-145 Unyielding Spirit`, `SFD-194 Counter Strike`, `VEN-025 Esteemed
Hierophant`, `VEN-126 Ki Barrier`; only the last is numbered.

On `OGN-241 Shen, Kinkou` the seven stops being single-target cover: 437.5.a sets lethal at Might 3 +
[Shield 2] + Prevent 7 = **12**, and 815.1.c.2 makes every other body of yours an invalid assignment
until Shen has it. Both cards are [Reaction], so the whole line is assembled on the opponent's turn.

## 3. `undercover-agent-karthus-empty-hand-draw` — 359.3.e.11, the discard is EFFECT text

**359.3.e.11, cited by nothing** (359.3.e.10 and 359.3.e.14.a both were):

> Instructions that can be partially followed are followed as much as possible and ignored otherwise.

and its example is this card's sentence — *"A player plays a spell that says 'Discard 2, then draw 2.'
If their hand is empty, the instruction to discard 2 will be ignored. They'll still draw 2."* **422.4
names the card outright**, ending *"Regardless of how many cards they discard, they then draw 2."*

The distinction that makes it work is one paragraph away: 422.3 refuses a discard **cost** that cannot
be completed, and this discard is not a cost — it sits after the [Deathknell] marker, which 808.1.c
makes *"functionally short for 'When I die, [Effect].'"* Nor is the draw linked to it under
359.3.e.14.a, because *"then draw 2"* references nothing the discard touched.

`OGN-236 Karthus, Eternal` doubles it. From an **empty** hand: four cards drawn, two held, two in the
trash. From a hand of two or more: four discarded and four drawn, hand size unchanged. **Chaos + Order
is exactly ONE legend name** — Heart of the Tempest (VEN-155 / VEN-197), census run against
`data/cards.json` — so this is a one-legend line with no second shell.

## 4. `curator-steel-paws-activated-ability-seven` — 377.2 and 377.2.a

**377.2 and 377.2.a were cited by nothing**; their sibling 377.2.b is the paragraph R30 was voted on.

> 377.2. Card text will refer to activating Activated Abilities with the word "use" or "play."

That is the paragraph that makes `VEN-145 Curator of the Sands`' own sentence legible — *"When you
**play** a unit, gear, or activated ability with Energy cost 7 or more"* — and 827.1 (*"Empower is an
Activated Ability keyword"*) with 827.1.c.1 supplies the other half. **Two catalogued Curator entries
read only her printed-cost half through 206 and neither opens this one.**

> 377.2.a. If "using" or "playing" an Activated Ability is part of a trigger condition, that condition
> is fulfilled when the Activated Ability resolves.

So the rebate lands **after** the payment: the two runes can never help pay for the ability that
produced them. That is the *engine offset by one event* shape this project already records for a 383.3.b
base cost and for 381 with 310.2, reached by a **third** route.

And it dictates the partner. 827.1.c.3 makes cost-altering text count *"when determining a card's
Empower cost for any reason"*. The pool has exactly **four** activated abilities reaching 7 Energy
(swept 2026-09-09): `VEN-043 Steel Paws` (flat 7), `VEN-046 Nasus, Ascended` (flat 8), and `VEN-032
Frostcoat Mother` and `VEN-050 Grumpy Rockbear`, both *"[Empower] 12 Energy. This ability costs 1 Energy
less for each rune you control."* 161.2.a fixes the Rune Deck at twelve and 315.3.b channels two a turn,
so **from the turn you control six runes those two stop triggering Curator** — the board state that lets
you afford them switches her off. All four sit inside her Calm/Mind identity; only the two flat ones are
hers to use.

## 5. `ashe-focused-scuttle-crab-hand-snapshot` — 424.3, 424.3.a, 424.3.a.1

**424.2.b, 424.3, 424.3.a, 424.3.a.1 and 424.4.a were all cited by nothing.** The teeth:

> 424.3.a.1. The Revealed state is applied to those cards. Any further cards added to that zone after
> the instruction has been executed do not become Revealed.

`UNL-169 Ashe, Focused` banishes *"a card revealed this way"*, so the choice is confined to the snapshot
424.3.a took (*"All cards currently in the specified zone"*) and a card drawn afterwards can never be
the target. The strip is exactly one card and cannot be re-aimed. 424.3's own example adds that a bare
reveal is **non-destructive and order-preserving** — *"return them to the top of the Main Deck in the
same order"* — so a reveal is not a mill and costs the revealer nothing; only the banish has a price.

The hole the family cannot see: 424.1 scopes revealing to a zone whose information is hidden, and
811.1.b hides a card **at a battlefield**, not in the hand — so **no hand reveal in the pool ever shows
a facedown card**. Swept 2026-09-09: six cards reveal an opponent's hand (`OGN-156 Sabotage`, `OGN-192
Mindsplitter`, `UNL-053 Scuttle Crab`, `UNL-135 Insightful Investigator`, `UNL-169 Ashe, Focused`,
`VEN-085 Decree of Strength`) and exactly **one** also opens the facedown: Scuttle Crab. The information
is bounded and small — 811.1.b allows one facedown per battlefield and 485.4 puts two on a Duel table —
which is why it is worth a card and not more. Calm + Order is **four** legend names, census run against
`data/cards.json`.
