## 1. `tricksy-tentacles-yuumi-forced-defend-subset` — 355.11.b, a group target degrades to a SUBSET

**355.11.b, cited by nothing** (355.11 and 355.11.a both were):

> If the group of targets no longer collectively fulfill the targeting restriction as the spell or
> ability resolves, that spell or ability's controller can choose a subset of the original targets that
> fulfills the targeting requirement for the spell or ability to affect.

Riot's worked example is a pool card, `OGN-256 Fox-Fire`, and a defensive pump — and it ends by drawing
the boundary of the subset:

> They can't choose to affect units at the same battlefield that weren't initially chosen as targets.
> They can, however, choose to affect units that were initially chosen as targets that left the chosen
> battlefield before Fox-Fire resolved as long as those units are all located at the same battlefield.

So **a pump aimed at a total-Might restriction costs you one dragged body, never the spell**, and a
defensive move does not dodge it either as long as the survivors end up co-located. The pool prints
three group targets priced by summed Might (swept over `data/corpus_flat.txt`, 2026-09-09): `OGN-256
Fox-Fire`, `UNL-054 Tricksy Tentacles`, `VEN-107 Decree of Discord`. 355.12 is what makes each member a
target in the first place.

The line: Tricksy Tentacles drags up to 8 total Might of enemy bodies onto a battlefield **you** control,
so 450 and 190.3.a.1 apply Contested status and 464.2.c.1 makes **them** the Attacker on **your** turn;
464.2.e.1 places the Defender's triggers last and 340.1 resolves them first, so Yuumi's +3 Might and
[Tank] land before the damage step. 466.5 gives Control only to a player who did not already have it, so
the class is ENGINE and no point is scored.

## 2. `ki-barrier-shen-kinkou-prevent-pool` — 437.3, a Prevent Value is spent down and survives

**Fifteen sub-rules of the Prevent block were cited by nothing**: 437.1, .1.a, .1.a.1, .1.b, .1.b.1,
.1.b.1.a, .1.b.1.b, .1.b.2, .2, .2.a, .3, .3.a, .3.b, .3.c and 437.6. (437.4, 437.5, 437.5.a, 437.5.b
and 437.7 were already carried by `void-gate-ki-barrier-prevent-counts-bonus-damage`, rc-walk-fc / #191,
which this entry cross-references and does not duplicate.)

The obvious reading of `VEN-126 Ki Barrier` is that its Prevent 7 is a one-shot spent by whatever it
meets first. **It is not.** 437.2 replaces the damage with one reduced by the tracked Prevent Value;
437.3 reduces that value by the amount prevented; 437.3.b makes the reduced number the newly tracked
value; 437.3.a expires it only *"If the Prevent Value is 0 or less."* A Prevent 7 that eats a Deal 2 is
a Prevent 5 and keeps working — **a pool for the turn**.

That makes the pool's two bounded Prevents **opposite tools**, which no entry had said. 437.1.b.2:
*"Prevent will always apply to the next damage that would be dealt to a unit affected by the Prevent
action."* `SFD-194 Counter Strike` reads *"The next time that unit would be dealt damage this turn,
prevent it"* — one instance of **any size**, the answer to `OGS-022 Final Spark` (8, the pool's largest
single Deal) and spent entirely by a 1-damage ping. Ki Barrier is the answer to a spell with several
Deal actions: against `OGN-248 Icathian Rain`, which prints *"Deal 2 to a unit."* six times, it absorbs
2 + 2 + 2 and 1 of the fourth — **seven of twelve**, where Counter Strike absorbs two. Counter Strike is
also Signature (Jax), so 103.2.d.2 forces a legend on any list that runs it; Ki Barrier is not.

437.3.c — *"If the Prevent Value is 'All' then it remains 'All.'"* — is why the other two Prevent cards
are different in kind and why the 437.3 arithmetic is visible on exactly one card. Four Prevent rows in
the pool, swept 2026-09-09: `OGN-145 Unyielding Spirit`, `SFD-194 Counter Strike`, `VEN-025 Esteemed
Hierophant`, `VEN-126 Ki Barrier`; only the last is numbered.

On `OGN-241 Shen, Kinkou` the seven stops being single-target cover: 437.5.a sets lethal at Might 3 +
[Shield 2] + Prevent 7 = **12**, and 815.1.c.2 makes every other body of yours an invalid assignment
until Shen has it. Both cards are [Reaction], so the whole line is assembled on the opponent's turn.

## 3. `undercover-agent-karthus-empty-hand-draw` — 359.3.e.11, the discard is EFFECT text

**359.3.e.11, cited by nothing** (359.3.e.10 and 359.3.e.14.a both were):

> Instructions that can be partially followed are followed as much as possible and ignored otherwise.

and its example is this card's sentence — *"A player plays a spell that says 'Discard 2, then draw 2.'
If their hand is empty, the instruction to discard 2 will be ignored. They'll still draw 2."* **422.4
names the card outright**, ending *"Regardless of how many cards they discard, they then draw 2."*

The distinction that makes it work is one paragraph away: 422.3 refuses a discard **cost** that cannot
be completed, and this discard is not a cost — it sits after the [Deathknell] marker, which 808.1.c
makes *"functionally short for 'When I die, [Effect].'"* Nor is the draw linked to it under
359.3.e.14.a, because *"then draw 2"* references nothing the discard touched.

`OGN-236 Karthus, Eternal` doubles it. From an **empty** hand: four cards drawn, two held, two in the
trash. From a hand of two or more: four discarded and four drawn, hand size unchanged. **Chaos + Order
is exactly ONE legend name** — Heart of the Tempest (VEN-155 / VEN-197), census run against
`data/cards.json` — so this is a one-legend line with no second shell.

## 4. `curator-steel-paws-activated-ability-seven` — 377.2 and 377.2.a

**377.2 and 377.2.a were cited by nothing**; their sibling 377.2.b is the paragraph R30 was voted on.

> 377.2. Card text will refer to activating Activated Abilities with the word "use" or "play."

That is the paragraph that makes `VEN-145 Curator of the Sands`' own sentence legible — *"When you
**play** a unit, gear, or activated ability with Energy cost 7 or more"* — and 827.1 (*"Empower is an
Activated Ability keyword"*) with 827.1.c.1 supplies the other half. **Two catalogued Curator entries
read only her printed-cost half through 206 and neither opens this one.**

> 377.2.a. If "using" or "playing" an Activated Ability is part of a trigger condition, that condition
> is fulfilled when the Activated Ability resolves.

So the rebate lands **after** the payment: the two runes can never help pay for the ability that
produced them. That is the *engine offset by one event* shape this project already records for a 383.3.b
base cost and for 381 with 310.2, reached by a **third** route.

And it dictates the partner. 827.1.c.3 makes cost-altering text count *"when determining a card's
Empower cost for any reason"*. The pool has exactly **four** activated abilities reaching 7 Energy
(swept 2026-09-09): `VEN-043 Steel Paws` (flat 7), `VEN-046 Nasus, Ascended` (flat 8), and `VEN-032
Frostcoat Mother` and `VEN-050 Grumpy Rockbear`, both *"[Empower] 12 Energy. This ability costs 1 Energy
less for each rune you control."* 161.2.a fixes the Rune Deck at twelve and 315.3.b channels two a turn,
so **from the turn you control six runes those two stop triggering Curator** — the board state that lets
you afford them switches her off. All four sit inside her Calm/Mind identity; only the two flat ones are
hers to use.

## 5. `ashe-focused-scuttle-crab-hand-snapshot` — 424.3, 424.3.a, 424.3.a.1

**424.2.b, 424.3, 424.3.a, 424.3.a.1 and 424.4.a were all cited by nothing.** The teeth:

> 424.3.a.1. The Revealed state is applied to those cards. Any further cards added to that zone after
> the instruction has been executed do not become Revealed.

`UNL-169 Ashe, Focused` banishes *"a card revealed this way"*, so the choice is confined to the snapshot
424.3.a took (*"All cards currently in the specified zone"*) and a card drawn afterwards can never be
the target. The strip is exactly one card and cannot be re-aimed. 424.3's own example adds that a bare
reveal is **non-destructive and order-preserving** — *"return them to the top of the Main Deck in the
same order"* — so a reveal is not a mill and costs the revealer nothing; only the banish has a price.

The hole the family cannot see: 424.1 scopes revealing to a zone whose information is hidden, and
811.1.b hides a card **at a battlefield**, not in the hand — so **no hand reveal in the pool ever shows
a facedown card**. Swept 2026-09-09: six cards reveal an opponent's hand (`OGN-156 Sabotage`, `OGN-192
Mindsplitter`, `UNL-053 Scuttle Crab`, `UNL-135 Insightful Investigator`, `UNL-169 Ashe, Focused`,
`VEN-085 Decree of Strength`) and exactly **one** also opens the facedown: Scuttle Crab. The information
is bounded and small — 811.1.b allows one facedown per battlefield and 485.4 puts two on a Duel table —
which is why it is worth a card and not more. Calm + Order is **four** legend names, census run against
`data/cards.json`.

## 23. `morgana-apothecary-hextech-ray-doubling-ladder` — 355.5.b, an ETB chooses AFTER the body lands

## 60. CORRECTION ONE — the 2v2 board has THREE battlefields, so the rate rises with the target

## 66. THE DEFECT — eight entries let a passive ability work from inside the Main Deck and none cites the paragraph that allows it

`SFD-175 Undertitan` (*"As I'm revealed from your deck, [Add] :rb_energy_2:."*) and `OGN-194 Nocturne,
Horrifying` (*"As you look at or reveal me from the top of your deck, you may banish me."*) are the pool's two
cards whose text does work while the card is **in the Main Deck**. Measured over `data/combos.json` at catalogue
761 and re-measured at **762** immediately before reporting, **eight entries** stand on that: `reksai-undertitan-reveal`, `nocturne-stacked-deck-cheat`,
`void-rush-undertitan-accelerate`, `diana-lunari-showdown-scry`, `teemo-strategist-undertitan-mind-order-reveal`,
`undertitan-pakaa-signpost-move-reveal`, `gemcraft-seer-nocturne-vision-replacement-deploy`,
`mystic-poro-nocturne-mono-chaos-look-package`.

All eight cite **370.1.b.1** — the paragraph that makes an *"as"* clause a Replacement Effect rather than a
trigger, and whose worked example is Undertitan by name. That citation is **true and incomplete**, which is
exactly the class §H19 predicted. It answers *what kind of ability this is* and says nothing about *why an
ability on a card nobody has played is doing anything at all.* Four paragraphs answer that, and **two of them
are cited by nothing in this project**:

> **365.1.** Passive Abilities of Permanents are typically only active while on the Board.

That is the DEFAULT, and on its own it kills the whole family. Cited seven times in the catalogue, never here.

> **366.1.** Passive Abilities of cards in zones that are outside of the Board will self-describe their context.
> Example: The passive ability "Play me only during an opponent's turn." applies in any zone from which that
> card can be played.
> Example: Undying Legion has a passive ability that reads “[Legion][>] You may play me from your trash for
> [3][C].” That passive ability only applies when Undying Legion is in the trash.

That is the OVERRIDE, and *"from your deck"* / *"from the top of your deck"* is the self-description. **It is
cited by exactly TWO entries out of 761** (`void-drone-sarcophagus-zone-discount`,
`immortal-phoenix-falling-star-cleanup-attribution`), neither of them in this family — and the card it names in
its own worked example, `UNL-025 Undying Legion`, is catalogued in `battering-ram-undying-legion-cards-played`,
which cites neither 366.1 nor 812.2.

> **368.1.** Passive Abilities can be Replacement Effects.

**Zero citations.** This is the hinge, and it is the paragraph a reader most needs: it is what makes 366.1 rather
than **385.1 / 385.2** the applicable rule. This project has 385.1 and 385.2 — *Triggered* Abilities outside the
Board — at 16 and 42 citations and has never had their passive counterpart, so the natural move for the next
reader is to reach for 385 and get the wrong block. 368.1 closes that in one sentence.

> **424.1.a.2.** Cards remain in the zone they are being Revealed from.
> Example: If a card is being Revealed from the top of a player's Main Deck, it is still the top card of that
> player's Main Deck.

That is why the self-described context is still satisfied **at the instant the passive fires**: revealing is not
a zone change (424.1.a: *"Revealed is a temporary state and is not a zone"*), so the Undertitan is on top of the
deck while it Adds. Cited six times overall and in **two** of the eight (`diana-lunari-showdown-scry`,
`teemo-strategist-undertitan-mind-order-reveal`).

**NO VERDICT IS WRONG, AND I CHECKED RATHER THAN ASSUMED.** The two cards demand opposite enablers — Undertitan
pays on a REVEAL only, Nocturne on a LOOK as well — and 424.1 (*"Revealing is the act of presenting a card to
all players"*) against 436.1 / 817.1.b (Predict and Vision are defined with *"look at"*) is the split. Every

`VEN-033 Pakaa Protector`, `OGN-121 Teemo, Strategist`, `UNL-079 Diana, Lunari`. Every Nocturne enabler looks:
`OGN-100 Gemcraft Seer` and `OGN-171 Mystic Poro` ([Vision]) and `OGN-183 Stacked Deck` (*"Look at the top 3"*).
**The authors got the distinction right without the paragraph** — which is precisely why no validator could ever
have surfaced this.

**UPGRADE, FLAGGED ONCE (§43):** add 368.1 + 366.1 against 365.1 to all eight, and 424.1.a.2 to the six that
lack it.

That is a Replacement Effect on a reveal, and *"then reveal those cards"* is a Game Action inside its own

> **373.2.a.1.** A Replacement Effect that replaces an event or Game Action that is part of another Replacement
> Effect will not interrupt the sequence of the replaced Replacement Effect’s application.

And the companion that makes it terminate, whose own worked example is a nested pair of Zhonya's Hourglasses:

> **370.2.** A Replacement Effect can only be applied once to an event, **or to any Game Actions or events that
> replace that event.**

`void-rush-undertitan-accelerate` runs `SFD-018` and `SFD-175` **in the same `uses[]`** and cites neither. The

## 68. A UNIT CANNOT BE COUNTERED, BY CONSTRUCTION — and the eleven counters in the pool prove it

Three paragraphs, none of the last two cited:

> **359.2.** A Permanent leaves the Chain and becomes a Game Object.
> **359.3.a.** This card becomes a Finalized Chain Item.
> **359.3.c.** Other players have an opportunity to play Reactions before the resolution of spells.

The pre-resolution Reaction window is scoped **to spells by its own words**, and 359.2 is why: a permanent is
never a Finalized Chain Item at all. 329.2 makes a Chain Item Pending only until the Check Legality step (358),
312.2.c withholds priority until every pending item finishes finalizing, and 330 ends the Chain the moment the
last item leaves it — so **playing a unit with no triggered ability gives the opponent no priority whatsoever**:
the state goes Closed while the card is Pending, the body arrives at 359.2, the Chain empties, and 310.1's
Neutral Open State returns priority to the turn player under 312.2.a. This is the denial lens (#100) read from
the other side: that lens says the opponent's only windows are Closed States and Showdowns, and 359.2 is the
paragraph that says **a unit play creates neither.**

Measured over `data/cards.json`, folded by name+type: **ELEVEN counter printings, and all eleven carry
[Reaction]** — `OGN-045 Defy`, `OGN-064 Wind Wall`, `SFD-045 Not So Fast`, `SFD-136 Hard Bargain`,
`SFD-206 Riposte`, `UNL-044 Flurry of Feathers`, `UNL-106 Repulse`, `UNL-131 Abandon`,
`UNL-190 Lilting Lullaby`, `VEN-039 Crumbling Sands`, `VEN-152 Rebuttal`. **Nine say "a spell". Exactly two say
"spell or ability"** — `SFD-045` (*"Counter an enemy spell or ability that chooses a friendly unit or gear"*)
and `UNL-106` (*"Counter an enemy spell or ability that chooses it and no other friendly unit"*). **ZERO name a
unit, a permanent or a card.**

Two consequences the catalogue has never stated:

- **An ETB-removal BODY is strictly harder to answer than a removal SPELL, and the gap is nine cards to two.**
  A removal spell is answerable by all eleven; the body cannot be stopped from arriving at all, and only its
  triggered ability is exposed — to the two cards above, and only when the trigger *chooses* something. Twelve
  BURST and CHAIN entries stand on attached Equipment and the #200 lane priced their answers in gear removal;
  nobody has priced the counter axis, where the answer is one card wide.
- **`VEN-069 Mel, Newly Awakened`'s *"Your spells and abilities can't be countered"* covers everything that was
  ever exposed and nothing that was not** — a unit never needed the clause, which is why she words it that way
  and why `VEN-015 Decree of Rage`, the pool's only other *"can't be countered"*, is a spell.

Domain note, members named: six of the eleven are Calm (`OGN-045`, `OGN-064`, `SFD-045`, `UNL-044`, `VEN-039`
mono-Calm; `UNL-190` Calm/Mind), two Chaos (`SFD-136`, `UNL-131`), and one each Body (`UNL-106`), Body/Order
(`SFD-206`) and Mind/Chaos (`VEN-152`). **Counterplay is a Calm mechanic**, on the same axis as the stun lens
(#61) and the denial lens (#100).

## 69. THE LIMITED / DISCRETIONARY CENSUS IS CLOSED, AND THERE ARE EXACTLY THREE DISCRETIONARY ACTIONS

§35 opened the two halves of rule 410 and listed a handful of members. The census can be closed outright,
because rules **413–446 label every Game Action in the game** with one of two sentences. Swept the rules file
for *"is a Limited Action"* / *"is a Discretionary Action"*: **34 classification lines, and exactly THREE are
Discretionary.**

- **Discretionary (3):** **419.2** playing a card, **420.3** the Standard Move inherent to Units, **421.2**
  Hiding.
- **Limited (31):** 413.2 Draw, 414.3, 415.3 Ready, 416.2 Recycle, **417.3 Deal Damage**, 418.3 Heal, 420.2 Move,
  422.2 Discard, 423.2 Stun, 424.2 Reveal, **425.2 Counter**, 426.2 Buff, **427.4 Banish**, 428.3 Kill, 429.4 Add,
  430.4 Channel, 431.4 Burn Out, 432.2 Double, 433.2 Swap, 434.2 Attach, 435.2 Detach, 436.2 Predict,
  **437.6 Prevent**, 438.2 Replace, 439.6 Create, 440.3 Burn, 441.3 Empower, 442.2 Disempower, 443.3 Skip,
  444.3 Pay, 446 Move.

Read with the paragraphs §22 opened — **312.1** (*"Priority is the singular exclusive right to take
Discretionary Actions"*), **410.1.a** (a Discretionary Action may be performed at any time during a player's
turn in a Neutral Open State) and **312.1.b.1** (*"Players can always take and make choices for Limited Actions
when instructed, regardless of Priority"*) — the whole timing model of this game collapses to one sentence:

> **There are three things a player may ever do AT WILL, and every keyword that widens timing ([Action],
> [Reaction], [Ambush]) is written against one of them. Everything else — thirty-one actions, from Drawing to
> Killing to Paying to Revealing to Countering — happens whenever an effect instructs it, on anybody's turn,
> with no priority and no keyword.**

That is the general form of at least six things this project derived card by card: an instructed recycle or
discard on the opponent's turn (§35), an instructed Deal on the opponent's turn, 444.2.c's rune payment inside a
finalization, Hiding being confined to your own Main Phase by 421.2 and nothing in 811, the Standard Move's
Main-Phase-only confinement, and the fact that a *move by effect* (420.2, Limited) escapes every restriction the
*Standard Move* (420.3, Discretionary) carries. **Eighteen of the 34 classification lines are cited by nothing**
— 413.2, 416.2, 418.3, 422.2, 423.2, 425.2, 427.4, 428.3, 429.4, 433.2, 434.2, 435.2, 436.2, 438.2, 439.6,
440.3, 442.2, 443.3 — but the value is the classification, not the lines, and the negative half is worth
stating because it is printed identically four times: 416.2.a, 422.2.a, 427.4.a, 437.6.a and 443.3.a all read
*"only when directed to by Game Effects"* / *"when instructed to do so"*. **No player may ever recycle, discard,
banish, prevent damage or skip anything as a voluntary act.**

## 74. 426.1.c SPLITS EVERY BUFF PAYOFF IN TWO, AND RIOT'S OWN EXAMPLE IS `SFD-047 SIMIAN ANCESTOR`'S SENTENCE

**This catalogue cites 702.3 four hundred and eighty-eight times and 702.3.a a hundred and thirty-one** — the
one-buff-per-unit cap is the most-cited fact in the project. **426.1.c, the paragraph that says what the cap
does to the rest of the sentence the buff was written in, is cited ONCE** (`blade-dancer-caitlyn-choose-ready`).

> **426.1.c.** Units with Buff Counters can still be chosen for actions that Buff units, but will not be Buffed
> as part of the execution.
> Example: A spell reads "Buff a unit. Then, if it was buffed this way, draw a card." It will place a buff
> counter on a unit that has no buff counter, and then its controller will draw a card. However, if the player
> chooses a unit with a buff counter on it already, then the card will not be drawn.
> Example: A unit reads “When you buff me, ready me.” If that unit is chosen for a game effect that would buff
> it and it is already buffed, its ability will not trigger.

**The second worked example is `SFD-047 Simian Ancestor` verbatim** — *"When you buff me, ready me."* — and
`simian-ancestor-arena-bar-wallop-ready` does not cite it. That entry reaches the right verdict through
702.3.a (*"if a Buff is added … on a Unit that already has a Buff, it is not placed instead"*), which is the
CAP; 426.1.c is the CONSEQUENCE, and it is the paragraph a reader checking that entry actually wants.

**The split, swept over `data/cards.json` and folded by name+type, and both families are tiny and exhaustive:**

- **"When you BUFF" — TWO cards, and 426.1.c kills both on an already-buffed body.** `OGN-152 Mistfall`
  (Body gear, *"When you buff a friendly unit, you may pay :rb_rune_body: and exhaust this to ready it"*) and
  `SFD-047 Simian Ancestor` (Calm, M5).
- **"When you CHOOSE" — FOUR cards, and 426.1.c keeps every one of them ALIVE**, because the already-buffed
  unit *"can still be chosen"*. `SFD-057 Irelia, Fervent` (Calm, *"When you choose or ready me"*),
  `SFD-142 Jae Medarda` (Chaos, *"When you choose me with a spell"* — spells only, per the #169 walk),
  `SFD-144 Spirit Wheel` (Chaos gear) and `SFD-195 Blade Dancer` (Calm/Chaos legend), the last two reading
  *"When you choose a friendly unit"*.

So on a board where every body already carries a buff, **a mass buffer stops being a buffer and becomes a pure
"choose N friendly units" engine**: every buff does nothing, every Mistfall and Simian trigger goes dark, and
every Spirit Wheel and Blade Dancer trigger fires exactly as before. CLAUDE.md records the positive half
(*"buffing is CHOOSING (702.2.a), so every mass buffer fires Blade Dancer / Irelia Fervent"*); 426.1.c is what
makes that survive saturation, which is the only board state where the distinction is worth anything.

**Two riders on the same paragraph.** 809.1.c prices [Deflect] *"for each time they choose"*, so you pay the
tax for choosing an already-buffed enemy — or your own Irelia — and receive no buff for it; and **426.1.b.2 is
the rules' own slot for the Lee Sin exception** (*"Some effects may grant a Game Object permission to be Buffed
multiple times. Such an effect ignores this restriction"*), which is a cleaner source than the rule-002
argument CLAUDE.md currently uses for `OGN-078 Lee Sin, Ascetic`'s *"I can have any number of buffs"*.
`lee-sin-buff-bank` already cites it; CLAUDE.md's sentence does not.

**UPGRADE, FLAGGED ONCE: 426.1.c to `simian-ancestor-arena-bar-wallop-ready`.**

## 75. NOTHING IN THIS POOL IS CREATED, SO EVERY TOKEN IS PLAYED — and that closes an escape hatch

> **439.1.** Creating is the act of producing a Game Object that previously did not exist in the game.
> **439.2.c.** The zone a Game Object is created to can be specified implicitly by the Game Action being
> performed.
> Example: “Play a 1 [M] Recruit token” specifies a zone where the token is Created to implicitly, by
> specifying the token is played and thus created on the chain.
> Example: “Create a 1 [M] Recruit token” does not specify a zone, so the Recruit token will be created at any
> location on the board that it can be played to.

Two arrivals, two different rule sets: a **played** token is on the Chain, is bound by 355.2.a (the base or a
battlefield you control) and is a play for 350.2, so it fires *"when you play a unit"*; a **Created** token
skips the Chain, takes 439.2.b.1's destination and fires nothing.

Measured over `data/corpus_flat.txt` on 2026-09-12: **the word "create" appears ZERO times in the pool**, and
**76 lines print "play a … token"**. The single card that puts a Game Object on the board without playing it
is `UNL-147 Baron Nashor` — *"As you play me, add the Baron Pit battlefield token to the board if it's not
there already"* — which is a BATTLEFIELD, so 439.2.b.5 rather than 439.2.b.1, and 355.2.a was never about it.

**So: every unit and gear token in this pool is PLAYED, with no exception.** The catalogue uses the positive
half of that constantly (350.2 + 185.2.b for a token firing a play trigger) and has never stated that it is
universal, which is what makes it an `excludes` fact rather than a coincidence: **no token can dodge a
prohibition or a watcher that bites on PLAYING.** `OGN-070 Mageseeker Warden` — the pool's biggest denial card,
whose text is a prohibition on playing units anywhere but the base — has no token-shaped leak, and the twenty-six
[Accelerate] units CLAUDE.md names as its one leak remain its only one. 439.2.b.1 goes live the moment a set
prints the word.

# Batch 17 — the gear pool is disjoint on the exhaust, which is why 718.2 has one target and five readiers have none

No entry. One structural measurement with four consequences, one first citation of the paragraph that decides
whether an attach CHOOSES, and one sourcing correction. Catalogue **762**.

## 79. NO EQUIPMENT IN THIS POOL TAPS, AND NO TAPPING GEAR IS EQUIPMENT

Measured over `data/cards.json`, folded by name+type, 2026-09-12: **107 gear names. 39 are Equipment and
ZERO of the 39 carry an exhaust symbol anywhere in their text. Of the 68 non-Equipment gear, 40 do.** The two
halves of the gear pool are disjoint on the exhaust, and Riot appears to have designed them that way.

Four things follow, and the first is the largest.

**(1) 718.2 and 721.2 have exactly ONE Equipment target in this pool.** Those two paragraphs — *"While in this
state, the card's printed Rules Text is Inactive"* and *"Inactive Abilities do not trigger, do not apply, and
cannot be activated"* — are cited 110 and 66 times here. What they can actually silence on an attached
Equipment is only whatever printed Rules Text it has besides its own [Equip], and swept over the 39, exactly
one Equipment has any: **`SFD-090 The Zero Drive`**, whose *":rb_energy_3::rb_rune_mind:, Banish this: Play all
units banished with this, ignoring their costs. (Use only if unattached.)"* is the reason that reminder is
printed on that card and on no other. (`UNL-188 Hextech Gauntlets` looks like a second hit and is not — its
second sentence is `[Effect]` text, a Conquer trigger, which 718.3 appends to the carrier rather than
silencing.)

**(2) CLAUDE.md's rule is enforced by the pool rather than by the designer.** The file records, from the
removal of `prize-of-progress-hexplate-equip-shuttle`, that *"a repeatable activated ability of a gear must be
a gear that is NEVER attached."* True — and the stronger statement is that **there was never a shuttle to
build**, because no Equipment in this pool has a repeatable activated ability to shuttle. The removed entry
was reaching for a card shape Riot has not printed.

**(3) No Equipment in this pool is ever EXHAUSTED, so every gear-readier is aimed at the other half.**
359.2.d — *"If it is a Non-Unit Gear, it enters the Board Ready at the player's Base"* — and 143.4 exhausts
units only; swept, **zero cards exhaust a gear you control** (every `exhaust` on a gear line in
`corpus_flat.txt` is that gear's own cost symbol). With (1), an Equipment has no cost to spend and nothing
spends it for you. So, re-swept rather than typed: **three cards ready a GEAR by name** — `VEN-149 Defender of
Tomorrow` (*"Ready a gear"* / *"Ready 2 gear"*), `VEN-150 Acceleration Gate` and `SFD-221 Veiled Temple` — and
**two more can reach one as "something exhausted"**, `OGN-162 Miss Fortune, Captain` (*"you may ready
something else that's exhausted"*) and `VEN-068 Jayce, Brilliant Inventor` (*"ready something besides me
that's exhausted"*), both of which name the exhausted state in their own text and therefore have **no legal
Equipment target at all**. `SFD-221 Veiled Temple` is the card that proves the split from the other side: its
two clauses are *"you may ready a friendly gear. If it's an Equipment, you may detach it"* — a ready half that
can only ever matter for the 40 tapping gear, and a detach half that is the only half addressed to the 39.

**(4) 434.5's worked example is dead letter here** — *"An exhausted equipment being attached to a unit does
not ready the equipment"* — since no Equipment in this pool reaches an attach in the exhausted state. The
paragraph's general half (*"Attaching a card to another card does not modify either card's state except in
making those cards Attached and the Top-Most Card, and causing the Attached card to change locations"*) is
still the source for the fact that an attach is not a state reset.

**THE SYNERGY LAYER ALREADY IMPLEMENTS THIS, AND I CHECKED RATHER THAN ASSUMED.** Both gear-ready rules in
`data/synergies.json` — `defender-of-tomorrow-readies-gear` and `veiled-temple-readies-gear` — carry
`partner.textMatches: ":rb_exhaust::"` with `types: ["gear"]`, so neither can match an Equipment. No defect,
and the measurement supplies the REASON the predicate is right, which the rules currently state only as a
predicate. `pit-crew-gear-ready` does match Equipment and is correct to: `OGN-091 Pit Crew` reads *"When you
play a gear, ready me"*, a PLAY trigger, and playing an Equipment is playing a gear.

## 80. 434.3 AND 435.3 ARE THE GENERAL HOME OF THE [Equip]-vs-[Weaponmaster] CHOOSING SPLIT

> **434.3.** Attaching cards does not inherently choose or specify a target. However, Game Effects that Attach
> cards may do so.
> **435.3.** Detaching cards does not inherently choose, or specify a target. However, Game Effects that
> Detach cards may.

Both cited by nothing. This project derives the consequence card by card from the two keyword paragraphs —
818.1.b.1 (the [Equip] ability's choice IS a Target, cited 7 times) against 821.1.c.6 (*"The Equip ability is
not activated this way, and the unit with the Weaponmaster ability is not chosen"*, cited 11) — and 434.3 is
the sentence that says why those two can differ at all: **the attach ACTION never chooses; only the effect
performing it can.**

The precision worth carrying: **a [Weaponmaster] attach chooses the GEAR and not the UNIT.** 821.1.c is
*"you may choose a Card you control with the Equipment tag"*, and 821.1.c.6 removes only the unit from the
choosing. So a paid [Equip] fires a *"when you choose a friendly unit"* payoff — `SFD-144 Spirit Wheel` and
`SFD-195 Blade Dancer`, the pair §74 swept — and a Weaponmaster attach fires neither, because the pool prints
**no** *"when you choose a gear"* payoff at all. That is the exact shape of the batch-16 buff split: the
action is the same and the choosing is not.

## 81. 435.1.c IS THE ONE-SENTENCE SOURCE FOR A REFINEMENT CLAUDE.md SOURCES TO THREE PARAGRAPHS

> **435.1.c.** The card being Detached has its Effect Text become Inactive and its Rules Text cease being
> Inactive.

CLAUDE.md records the [Equip] one-way door and its 2026-09-07 refinement — *"it is a one-way door out of the
ATTACHMENT, not out of the game"* — and sources that to 718.1 read with 719.5 and 457.1. 435.1.c says it
outright and in the Detach block where a reader would look for it, together with its two companions 435.1.d

(re-attach, which 434.1.f makes a detach in the same action) and `SFD-221 Veiled Temple`. **435.1.a.1**, also
uncited, is the cheap guard on all four: *"Game effects that instruct a player to Detach a card that is
currently not Attached to anything will do nothing."*

# Batch 18 — why the Undertitan could not have been written as a trigger, and the thirteen spells that never brick

## 84. 384 IS CITED BY NOTHING WHILE ITS EXCEPTION IS CITED 82 TIMES — and together they explain a design choice

Exactly the shape §66 found on the passive side, one block along. Measured on 2026-09-12: **384, 384.1 and
384.2 have ZERO citations, while 385.1 and 385.2 have 32 and 50.** This project cites the exception eighty-two
times and has never cited the rule it is an exception to.

> **384.2.** Triggered Abilities of Permanents are only able to have their Conditions evaluated while on the
> Board.
> **385.1.** Triggered Abilities on cards outside of the Board rely on the Information Level of the zone they
> are in.
> **385.2.** Triggered Abilities outside of the Board will self-describe their context.

Set that beside the passive pair from §66 and **one word is different, and it is load-bearing**: 365.1 says a
passive is *"**typically** only active while on the Board"*, 384.2 says a trigger's Condition can *"**only**"*
be evaluated there. The passive default is soft and the triggered default is hard, and the trigger's escape
hatch carries a condition the passive's does not — **385.1's Information Level**, whose reference table is
also uncited:

> **355.10.a.1.** Public zones are Battlefield Zones, Bases, Trashes, Legend Zones, Champion Zones, and
> Facedown Zones.

**The Main Deck and the Hand are not on that list.** So the answer to a question §66 raised and did not
answer — why is `SFD-175 Undertitan`'s *"As I'm revealed from your deck, [Add] :rb_energy_2:"* written as a
replacement effect rather than as the obvious *"When I'm revealed from your deck"* trigger? — is that **it
could not have been a trigger.** 384.2 bars the Condition from being evaluated off the board, 385.2's escape
requires the ability to self-describe its context, and 385.1 makes that escape depend on the Information
Level of the zone — and the Main Deck is secret. A replacement effect is a PASSIVE (368.1), and the passive
escape at 366.1 asks only for self-description with no Information Level clause. The card is written the only
way it could be.

**Swept over `data/cards.json` for cards whose own text acts on themselves in a non-board zone, folded by
name+type — twenty printings, and the split is exactly along that line:**

- **Triggered, and both are in the TRASH, which 355.10.a.1 makes public:** `OGN-037 Immortal Phoenix`
  (*"When you kill a unit with a spell, you may pay :rb_energy_1::rb_rune_fury: to play me from your trash"*)
  and `OGN-252 Super Mega Death Rocket!` (*"When you conquer, you may discard 1 to return this from your
  trash to your hand"*), which is 385.2's own worked example.
- **Passive, also in the trash:** `UNL-025 Undying Legion`, which is 366.1's own worked example, plus the
  **fourteen [Flow] spells** whose reminder reads *"You may play this from your trash for its Flow cost"* —
  `VEN-003`, `VEN-012`, `VEN-031`, `VEN-049`, `VEN-051`, `VEN-081`, `VEN-100`, `VEN-105`, `VEN-116`,
  `VEN-127`, `VEN-140`, `VEN-148`, `VEN-154`, `VEN-156` — where 366.2 / 366.2.a (a cost-altering passive
  applies in any zone the card can be played from) is what makes a Flow cost payable from a zone the card is
  not played from normally. `UNL-186 Death from Below` is the hybrid: a reflexive trigger (387.1) on the
  board that grants the trash play.
- **Passive replacement, in the MAIN DECK, and only these two:** `SFD-175 Undertitan` and `OGN-194 Nocturne,
  Horrifying`.
- **From the HAND: nothing at all.** Every *"from your hand"* instruction in the pool belongs to a card
  already on the board.

**The rule that falls out is worth more than the census: the trash can carry a trigger, the deck can only
carry a passive, and the hand carries neither.** That is an `excludes` fact for any future line — and it is
also the reason the two deck-dwelling cards are immune to every trigger multiplier in the pool, which the
Undertitan entries already state from the 370.1.b.1 side without knowing why the card had to be built that
way.

## 85. 355.13 NAMES THE SPELLS THAT CAN NEVER BRICK, AND CLAUDE.md DEFINES THAT CLASS ONLY BY ITS FAILURES

> **355.13.** If a card specifies that a player chooses “any number” or “up to” some number of Game Objects to
> be affected, they may choose any number of available targets, including zero. If they choose zero, the spell
> or ability can be played without any targets.

Zero citations. CLAUDE.md carries the negative half of this as one of its hardest-won lessons, from the #166
cross-audit: *"filler must be unconditionally castable, and a targeting spell never is"* — the case being
`swain-shurelya-double-conquer`, whose spell leg was `VEN-061 Decree of Insight` (*"Give an enemy Body unit
−5 Might"*), unplayable against a board with no Body unit, so all six Swain points evaporated. 358.1 checks
that all chosen targets are legal and 358.5 undoes the attempt, which is why that leg simply cannot be played.
**355.13 is the paragraph that names the exception class, and the class has members.**

Swept over `data/cards.json` for spells printing *"up to"* or *"any number of"*: **seventeen printings.**
**Thirteen are unconditionally castable** because every choice in them carries the qualifier (or, for
`UNL-103 Disposal Order`, because its second mode is *"Draw 1"*):

`OGN-105 Singularity` (Mind E6 P2), **`OGN-224 Salvage` (Order E2 P1)**, `OGN-256 Fox-Fire` (Calm/Mind E3),
`OGN-264 Guerilla Warfare` (Mind/Chaos E2 P1), `OGS-011 Flash` (Chaos E2), `SFD-043 Emperor's Divide`
(Calm E2), `SFD-080 Bellows Breath` (Mind E1 P1), `SFD-198 Arise!` (Calm/Order E6 P1),
`UNL-054 Tricksy Tentacles` (Calm E4 P1), `UNL-103 Disposal Order` (Body E2),
`VEN-103 Shadows of the Past` (Chaos E3 P1), `VEN-107 Decree of Discord` (Chaos E1 P1),
`VEN-150 Acceleration Gate` (Mind/Body E3 P1).

**And FOUR contain an "up to" and still brick, which is the trap the lesson is really about** — the tell is
whether any choice in the sentence lacks the qualifier: `SFD-023 Piercing Light` (*"Deal 2 to **a unit** at a
battlefield**, then** deal 2 to up to one other unit"*), `SFD-184 Relentless Pursuit` (*"**Move a friendly
unit.** You may attach up to one Equipment…"*), `UNL-198 Moonfall` (*"**Choose a battlefield where you have
units.** You may move up to one enemy unit…"*) and `VEN-140 Shuriken Flip` (*"Deal 2 to up to one enemy unit
at a battlefield, **then move a friendly unit**"*).

**The best filler in the pool is `OGN-224 Salvage`, and it is a second thing at the same time.** Order, 2
Energy + 1 Power: *"You may kill up to one gear. Draw 1."* By 355.13 it is castable into an empty board, by
419.4.b it counts as a card played for every non-triggered check even if every instruction fizzles, and it
replaces itself. It is also one of the sixteen gear-removal printings #200 measured against the twelve BURST
and CHAIN entries that stand on attached Equipment — so the cheapest unconditional filler in Order is the
same card as that identity's answer to an Equipment finisher. Every one of the seventeen is already in at
least one entry, so there is no row here; what there is, is a list a future author can pick from instead of
re-deriving the lesson from a broken leg.
