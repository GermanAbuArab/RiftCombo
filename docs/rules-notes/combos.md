# Combo authoring rules

> Moved verbatim from `CLAUDE.md` by #274 (2026-10-06). Read before authoring, promoting or auditing any entry in `data/combos.json`.

## Combos
- `data/combos.json` is AUTHORED. `status: verified` requires a hand-walked loop against card text + Core Rules with sources; agent output enters as `candidate` until a human reviews it.
- Every combo carries a class: INFINITE (walked, unbounded), BURST (**one** scoring event reaches 8), CHAIN (reaches 8 across **several** scoring events, without the opponent taking a turn in between), ALT_WIN (a card that literally says you win), or ENGINE. Never label INFINITE without a "repeat" step. CHAIN was added 2026-09-04 (issue #20) for the three Time-Warp-chained and double-Conquer finishers that reach 8+ but not in one event: labelling them ENGINE would have been false, since they win. The class is a plain string in the UI (`c.class.replace("_", " ")`), but it is typed in `src/types.ts` and ranked in `src/combos.ts` CLASS_RANK plus the sort orders in `web/graph.ts` and `web/main.ts` — all five must be updated together.
- Do not accept structural verdicts from agents ("no X exists in the pool") without walking the validation set first; the first hunt's "no infinite combos" was wrong.
- Authoring traps the 2026-09-03 REFUTE passes caught repeatedly, check every new entry against them: entering an EMPTY enemy battlefield is not an attack (Assault and "When I attack" need the Attacker designation, which exists only inside a Combat with a garrison: 807.1.d, 383.4.e, 461); Energy added in Awaken or the Beginning Phase is lost when the Rune Pool empties at the Main Phase start (167) — but NOT an [Add] scheduled "at the start of your next Main Phase", because 316.2 orders the Main Phase tasks and 316.3's sweep runs BEFORE 316.4's start-of-Main-Phase effects (refined by #161, 2026-09-06; Blue Sentinel's rainbow is live Power); and **the pool has exactly THREE trigger multipliers** — a sweep of all 935 base codes for "trigger an additional time" returns `OGN-236 Karthus, Eternal` (Deathknell), `UNL-029 Red Brambleback` (conquer here) and `UNL-087 Blue Sentinel` (hold here), all three catalogued — so no BURST in that family can be scaled by a different doubler, only by copies (103.2.b caps at 3) or a denser payoff, and `VEN-138 Shen, Leader of the Kinkou Order` ("exactly one other unit you control here") is the one hold payoff the Sentinel cannot be stacked on, since the second Sentinel is a second other unit and 383.2.a.1 keeps his trigger off the Chain (#161); a Repeat gives no window to re-exhaust a resource permanent between executions (429.3, R21); Temporary tokens die before Scoring, so they never Hold and never see a Main Phase start (816.1.b) — but that trap does NOT stop a Temporary body being COUNTED by an ability that fires at the same instant, because 816.1 makes Temporary a Triggered Ability and 383.3.d lets the player controlling both abilities order them (settled 2026-09-04 for `gutter-palace`, which had it filed as an open reading); Gold tokens are printed "exhausted", but Renata Industrialist overrides that and they enter ready (R25 = A); recycling a rune for Power sends it to the Rune Deck (161.2.b), so Ekko readies only what is still on the board; a recall is not a move (456) and does not change state (458). A `would die` shield of the heal / exhaust / recall family ERASES the Deathknell of what it saves: 808.1.d.1 removes the triggered ability from the chain when the permanent never reaches the Trash (found 2026-09-06 on `soraka-fiora-loop-shield`, whose replacement has no `may`, so it also switches off the Deathknell of any friendly body under 4 Might that dies at the base). **A token never reaches the trash**: 186 / 186.1 — tokens exist only on the board or the chain and cease to exist the moment they enter any other Non-Board Zone — so a `Recycle 1 from your trash` (Vi, Destructive) can never pick a dead Recruit, Gold or Bird, and a loop that counts on recycling its tokens is wrong on its face; 416.6 makes the recycling player choose WHICH cards a `Recycle X from [Zone]` takes, and it does not target them (found 2026-09-06 walking #62's contradictions). And the shorter form of the same trap, rule 185 — **"Tokens are not cards."** — with 416.1 (Recycling is defined on CARDS only): a line that recycles a token, or feeds a `Recycle N` cost with tokens, is refuted on its face; two sources in #62 believed Mech tokens were recycle fuel. **A Recall comes from ANYWHERE** (455: "relocated from anywhere to its Base without it being a Move"), so a save-by-recall like Guardian Angel also switches off any `While I'm at a battlefield` text on what it saves.
- A BURST entry must reach 8 **with the quantities it declares**, and the class must match `terminatesIn`. Check the entry's own formula against its own `uses` before authoring or promoting: the 2026-09-04 audit of all eleven BURSTs found four that failed this. `ahri-blue-sentinel-hold`, `blue-sentinel-trinity-force-hold` and `ivern-sentinel-hold` each declared a board their own printed formula resolves to 7; `tryndamere-brambleback-conquer` declared one worth 4, which its own REFUTE note had already flagged in 2026-09-03 without the fix ever being applied; and `renata-time-warp-ekko-refresh` claimed BURST while its own `terminatesIn` described points spread over two turns (reclassed ENGINE). A REFUTE verdict in `notes` is not a fix — grep for corrections that were diagnosed and never applied.
- Recycle budget is the real currency of an INFINITE loop, not the 3-copy deck limit. Recycle (416.1) puts cards on the BOTTOM OF THE DECK, so a spell consumed every pass is fine as long as the entry can name the recycle slot AND the matching draw that bring it back. The verified `lux-infinite-energy` engine saturates its 4 draws per pass and leaves EXACTLY ONE spare Forge recycle slot (Forge recycles 3 of its 4; Ekko's Deathknell recycles itself). REFINED 2026-09-04 by the joint ledger on issue #21 (`docs/phase0/walks/2026-09-04-loop-budget-ledger.md`): the scarce resource per pass is **draws, not slots**. Both pass types leave spare slots and ZERO spare draws — the Energy pass leaves 1 slot, and `lux-infinite-power` is a second, different pass that leaves 2. And the draw is purchasable: Renata Mastermind's `1 Energy + 1 Mind: Draw 1` carries NO exhaust in its cost (unlike her Score ability beside it), so extra draws are unlimited and an extra cycled card costs 1 Energy + 1 Mind Power ≈ 10 Energy at the 9-Energy-per-Power rate. No payoff in the catalogue consumes a card per PASS; they consume one per point, and a point costs ~50 passes, so ~50 spare slots accumulate per point. The three Renata routes are alternatives because they all do the same job (ready Renata to score again), not because the engine cannot feed two. Do not re-derive any of this as a copy-limit bound. **Equipment that copies text COMPOSES, it does not add** (settled 2026-09-06 on #45): 434.1.c gives the Top-Most Card ALL the Effect Text of ALL its attached cards; 477.2.c appends that text in Layer 2, 479.1 finds no dependency between two copies that each alter the other, 480.3 falls back to timestamp order (attach order — **and 434.1.b.1 is why that parenthesis must not be read as the physical pile**, catalogue 13, this file 0 until 2026-09-14: *"In the situation where there is more than one card attached to the Top-Most card, they should be stacked in such a way that all Effect Text boxes and Might Bonuses are readable. **The order of the Attached cards has no bearing on the application of effects**."* **Two different orderings and the file carried one**: 480.3 orders by TIMESTAMP, 434.1.b.1 makes the stack presentation, so re-stacking the pile changes nothing and a player cannot re-sequence a composition by tidying it. The same paragraph is load-bearing arithmetic from the other side — it *contemplates more than one card attached to a Top-Most card*, which is what lets three Trinity Forces stack on one body so T is not tied to the body count), and 476.1 applies each effect only once across all sequences — so v Svellsongur on one unit yield 2^v instances, not 1+v, and 476.1 is why R6 = A opens no recursion. `svellsongur-copy-hold` went from 11 to 17 points on the same five cards and its ceiling from 26 to 41. Companion fact from the same walk: an ability worded `When I conquer or hold` is BOTH a Conquer Effect and a Hold Effect (823.1.b, 823.1.c.1 spell it out for Hunt), so Reckoner's Arena doubles it twice — `ivern-arena-sentinel-hold` reaches 9 for 20 Energy + 2 Power. Always count the [Equip] costs and the Power: two published "18 Energy" figures had counted neither. **A legend is not at a location** (107.4.b: the Legend Zone "is not a location"), so Reckoner's Arena's "here" and Svellsongur's attach never reach a legend's own `When you conquer` text — the whole "legend + Arena" family is dead on arrival (#47, 2026-09-06). And 206 / 206.1 make cost-triggered abilities (Lady of Luminosity, Curator of the Sands — both are the worked examples) fire on the PRINTED or BASE cost, so discounts and free plays do not switch them off. REFINED by #47's walk (2026-09-06): that holds for CARDS (206 has no exception), but 206.1 for ABILITIES says "unless otherwise specified", and Empower specifies — 827.1.c.3: cost-altering text "is taken into account when determining a card's Empower cost for any reason" — so an Empower discounted to 0 does NOT fire a cost-threshold trigger; the "Empower discounted to 0 that still triggers on its base cost" family is dead. **AND THE INVERSE IS PRINTED TWO PARAGRAPHS AWAY, SO STATE THE PAIR OR THE NEXT READER GENERALISES THE WRONG HALF (added 2026-09-14; 356.4.f catalogue 6, 356.4.f.1 catalogue 7, this file 0 each).** **356.4.f**: *"Discounts can reduce additional costs, including to 0."* **356.4.f.1**: *"An optional additional cost was 'paid' if the player made the decision to pay it. **It doesn't matter how much the player actually paid**"* - and Riot's worked example is `OGN-044 Clockwork Keeper`, in the pool, paying an optional `[C]` reduced to 0 and STILL DRAWING. **So an optional additional cost discounted to 0 is still PAID and still fires its payoff, while an Empower discounted to 0 stops COSTING and fires nothing - because 356.4.f.1 asks about a DECISION and 827.1.c.3 asks about a NUMBER.** That reaches every *"if you paid the additional cost"* card in the pool, and a catalogue entry works it: *"The cost reaches 0. 356.4.f allows that, and 356.4.f.1 keeps it 'paid' because the decision to pay it was made."* Two more facts from the same walk: Heimerdinger copies ACTIVATED abilities with exhaust in the cost, never a "you may exhaust me to…" (that is a Triggered Ability, 383.1, and triggers are not copied); a token with no printed destination goes to the base OR a battlefield you control (355.2.a) — no walk needed; and "Legend" in card text means the legend in the Legend Zone (355.9.a.4), which is how Heimerdinger reaches a legend's exhaust ability. **Tokens are born at the base, and points are scored at battlefields** (#48, 2026-09-06): of the 49 cards that create unit tokens, 18 say "at your base" outright, 13 say "here" (a battlefield), and 18 name no destination (355.2.a: base, or a battlefield you control). Exactly ONE card in the pool relocates tokens in bulk — `SFD-177 Azir, Sovereign`, "When I attack, you may move any number of your token units to this battlefield" — so the bottleneck of every token line is the walk to the battlefield, not the token count. And there is NO window to add a body at the instant of the Hold: 315.2.b.2 puts the Hold in the Scoring Step of the Beginning Phase, 312.2.a gives priority only in the Main Phase, and 383.2.a.1 measures the condition when the trigger is placed — the seven for the Plaza must survive the opponent's whole turn. Four more from the token walk (#48, 2026-09-06): **"here" is read at execution** (359.3.f.2), so an effect that plays tokens "here" twice on two landings leaves each batch where it fell — two Signpost/Accelerate landings do NOT stack on the Plaza, only the last one counts; **the Plaza count includes the body that conquered it** — 355.2.a needs you to control the Plaza before the tokens are born and 323.6 needs it still occupied, so Corina's line is 8 there, not 7, and `flurry-of-feathers-grand-plaza-win` already counted that way; **Gold born from a Hold is next turn's Power** — Awaken (315.1) precedes the Scoring Step, the Gold token's ability costs an exhaust (187.5), so a Hold-made Gold enters exhausted unless Renata Glasc, Industrialist is out (R25 = A); and **check 103.1.b.1 before folding a card into an existing entry** — #48 proposed Chemtech Cask (Mind) inside an Order/Chaos entry, impossible by Domain Identity. **Stun lens (#61, 2026-09-06):** ZERO cards exhaust an enemy unit (full inventory of the verb, 32 uses; Conscription steals first), and exhausting is not stunning — "exhaust" appears in no combat rule 461–466, 464.2.c.3 and 465.2.b do not filter by state, so an exhausted defender fights with its full Might; what makes a stunned unit PAY is not 423.1.b (which only removes its contribution to combat damage) but 423.1.c read with 710: it still needs damage equal to its full CURRENT Might to die, which is why Leona Zealot's −8 matters (corrected by #89's walk). 423.1.a.1 "A Stunned Unit can not be Stunned again" plus 423.1.a.2 (cleared only at cleanup 3d) means no stun engine can be INFINITE; no card pays points for a stun, so the lens yields no BURST/CHAIN/ALT_WIN; stun is Calm + Order (21 of 29 lines, none in Mind or Fury). A hidden 0-Energy stun caps at one per CONTROLLED battlefield per turn (811.1.b) and must target there (811.1.d.2). **The mechanic lenses are NOT all swept** — the 2026-09-06 tournament pass (#89) showed at least four more with real play behind them: DISCARD and recursion from the trash (35 `discard` + 47 `from your trash` lines; two of #89's biggest candidates live there, and 422.1.b makes "When I am discarded" execute AFTER the discard, i.e. from the trash), BUFF (49 lines; the 258-list Boss + Showstopper + Sett group comes from it and sits on 808.1.d.1), Might REDUCTION (Leona Zealot's −8 to stunned units is in no entry and corrects #61's coverage; Fox-Fire and Ahri's −Might), and "cards played this turn". **Ready lens (#56, 2026-09-06):** "enters ready" is not a ready — 143.4 makes units enter exhausted and 415.1 defines Readying as marking a Game Object already on the board, so the 48 "enters ready" cards can never give an exhaust back; only 16 cards in the pool ready something other than themselves. A legend IS on the board (107.4.c, "The Champion Legend here is a Game Object") even though it is not at a location (107.4.b), so readies reach it: five cards ready your legend (SFD-039, SFD-210, OGN-162 Miss Fortune, VEN-068 Jayce, Brilliant Inventor's "ready something besides me that's exhausted", and OGN-111 via Heimerdinger's copy) — #56's walk added Jayce after #56's hunt had counted four. Five cards ready gear (VEN-149, VEN-150, SFD-221, OGN-162, VEN-068) — **and NONE of the five can ready an EQUIPMENT, which this line did not say (corrected 2026-09-12, #187 batch 17).** Measured over `cards.json` folded by name+type: of 107 gear names, the **40 Equipment carry ZERO exhaust symbols anywhere in their text** while 40 of the other 67 do — the two halves of the gear pool are disjoint on the exhaust. With 359.2.d entering a non-unit gear READY at base, 143.4 exhausting units only, and zero cards in the pool exhausting a gear you control, **no Equipment in this pool is ever exhausted**, and 415.1 defines Readying on something that is. Three of the five name a GEAR (VEN-149, VEN-150, SFD-221) and two name *"something exhausted"* (OGN-162, VEN-068), and the split is proved from the card side by `SFD-221 Veiled Temple` itself, whose two clauses are *"you may ready a friendly gear. **If it`s an Equipment, you may detach it**"* — the ready half can only ever matter for the 40 that tap, and the detach half is the only half addressed to Equipment. Same measurement kills three other things: 718.2 and 721.2 are cited 110 and 66 times here and have exactly ONE Equipment target in the pool (`SFD-090 The Zero Drive`, the only one of the 40 with printed Rules Text beyond its own Equip clause, which is why it alone prints *"use only if unattached"*); 434.5`s worked example about an exhausted Equipment being attached is dead letter; and the standing rule that a repeatable activated gear ability must live on a gear that is never attached **is enforced by the POOL rather than by the designer — there was never a shuttle to build** — a `grep` that swallowed an adjective once published "the ONLY card that readies gear" inside a verified entry; when a note claims uniqueness, grep the whole pool with `-i` and the bare word before shipping it. **Movement lens (#58, 2026-09-06):** `[Ganking]` is NOT an extra move — 810.1.c.3: "It does not give additional abilities or activations of Movement, only new options for the Standard Move" (37 corpus lines carry it). 144.4 restricts destinations and 420.3.a puts the exhaust cost ONLY on the Standard Move, so a move BY EFFECT goes battlefield to battlefield without Ganking and moves EXHAUSTED units — that is the mechanical name of the token bottleneck from #48 (`UNL-045 Forgotten Signpost` does it for 0). And no entry of the catalogue moves an ENEMY unit: emptying the garrison wins the combat with no damage step (465.1 needs both Attacking and Defending units for the damage tasks; 466.5.d makes establishing Control a Conquer), while 323.9 marks no Combat when no opposing units are present, so the "empty battlefield is not an attack" trap is respected — it is a Conquer without an Attacker. REFINED by the #58 walk (2026-09-06), which found the rule the hunt had missed: **323.10** ("If Units of two opposing players are no longer present at a Battlefield that has a Combat Staged **before it has opened**, the Combat will cease being Staged") opens a THIRD route — evacuate a garrison after the Combat is staged and it un-stages itself, leaving only the Showdown, so 348.2.a / 348.2.a.1 give Control and a Conquer with no damage and no Attacker designation. **Only a Triggered Ability reaches that window**: while its trigger sits on the chain the state is Neutral Closed (310.2) and 323.13 (which needs a *Neutral Open* State) cannot open the Combat, whereas after a plain move the very next Cleanup stages the Combat (323.9) and opens it (323.13) with no gap — and 155 bars a spell without [Action]/[Reaction] from a Showdown. So an evacuation SPELL must be cast **before** the entering move, never after; `charm-evacuate-conquer` and `faefolk-challenger-forced-attacker` are two different lines for that reason, not two versions of one. Companion facts from the same walk: 323.6 strips a player's Control the moment their last body leaves (a *staged* Combat is not an *ongoing* one), so pulling the garrison off makes the battlefield uncontrolled — i.e. **open** — in the same Cleanup; 190.3.a / 190.3.a.1 / 450 key Contested on the moved **unit's controller**, not on who caused the move, so dragging an enemy body onto your battlefield makes THEM the Attacker on YOUR turn (464.2.c.1); and 464.2.e.1 puts the Defender's triggers on the Combat Chain last, which by 340.1 makes them resolve FIRST. Two paraphrases to distrust: `447.2` is only the definition of Destination (the invalid-destination list is 447.2.a / 447.2.b), and `190.4.c` ends "unless there is a Combat or Showdown ongoing there", a clause the #58 issue body dropped. `SFD-079 Bard, Mercurial` is the pool's only bulk mover of units of ANY kind (Azir Sovereign moves only tokens, and needs an enemy garrison to attack) and it needs the opposite board: an UNCONTROLLED battlefield, which 190.1 makes both of them at the start of a Duel. REFINED AGAIN 2026-09-06 by the #44 walk: **every deck has a free floor of 2 Power per turn**, so measure any new Power source against it, not against zero — 164.2.b (`Recycle this: [Reaction] — Add [C]`) carries no exhaust, so a rune already exhausted for Energy still recycles for Power; 161.2.b sends it to the Rune Deck, not the Main Deck; and 315.3.b / 430.4.a channel 2 back every Channel Phase before the Main Phase. The Henge is E2 P1, so the FIRST pass must be primed with the Power the conversion will later supply. **Burn Out, the three rules every draw line must respect (#59, 2026-09-06):** drawing past the deck burns out (431.1.a) and so does milling — `[Burn N]` or any "put cards from your Main Deck" past the deck (431.1.b) — but LOOKING or REVEALING past the deck never does (431.1.c: "does not Burn Out"), so reveal-and-draw is the only free family inside a loop that runs with the Main Deck empty. The "0 spare draws" of the Lux ledger is a WALL, not a slack to widen: with the deck empty, the 4 draws and the 4 cards that come back are balanced on purpose and a fifth draw is 431.1.a — **and it is NOT one point you may choose to pay, which is how this file read it until 2026-09-12 (#187/300-499 lane)**: inside a loop the trash is empty BY CONSTRUCTION, because 416.1 recycles to the bottom of the DECK and never to the trash, so 431.2.b has nothing to refill with and 431.3.a follows verbatim — *"Unless some effect intervenes, this will result in them burning out repeatedly, giving 1 point to an opponent each time, until an opponent passes the Victory Score and wins the game"* — with 431.3.b making every point after the first unpreventable (*"Points gained after the first Burn Out being processed in sequence cannot be replaced or prevented by any means"*). The fifth draw is not a price, it is the game — every extra draw source needs a recycled card riding with it. A draw with exhaust in its cost is useless inside a loop (every pass is the same turn) and no repeatable gear-reader exists, which kills 13 of the 102 uncatalogued draw cards including Garbage Grabber. **Discard/trash lens (#95, 2026-09-06):** a card's own trigger works from the trash — 385.1/385.2 (Triggered Abilities outside the Board self-describe their context; 385.2's worked example is the "return this from your trash" wording of Super Mega Death Rocket!) — and neither 385 nor 422 was cited by any entry before. Five measured facts: negative Might READS as 0 for spells, abilities and combat damage but IS NOT 0 (143.2.b / 143.2.b.1), so reverting it costs the real value; a `to a minimum of 1` floor is snapshotted when it applies and floors do not stack (477.3.b) — the nine Origins reductions carry a floor, the fourteen from SFD/UNL/VEN do not; increases apply first and decreases LAST (477.3.e.2.a), so a −Might always wins the race; doubling a negative Might adds +0 (477.3.c, worked example Last Stand + Eclipse); and `[Repeat]` gives EXACTLY one extra execution (820.1.b, 820.1.c.3), which may pick a different target (820.2.a) — Frigid Touch, Feral Strength, Bonds of Strength and Danger Zone are not uncapped engines. Nothing converts Might size into points except OGN-034's excess threshold of 5, so the lens yields ENGINE, never BURST.
