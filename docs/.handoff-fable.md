## What we're building

A combo finder + visualizer for **Riftbound TCG** (Riot's League of Legends card game).
Paste a decklist or deck code -> see every known combo in it, rendered as a graph.

Modeled on **LOOPLINE** (loopline.robiichi.workers.dev), a Japanese MTG tool that cross-references
a Moxfield deck against Commander Spellbook and draws the combos as a graph.

**The core asymmetry, which drives everything:** LOOPLINE is a thin visualization layer over a
database somebody else spent six years building. Riftbound has no such database. We are building
the database AND the tool, and the database is the hard 90%.

## Phase 0 verdict: gate FAILED for the planned architecture, and found a better one

**What works — keep it:**
- Structured extraction: 921/921 cards, 0 schema violations, faithful primitives
- The rules primer, validation set, and refutation checklist

**What failed — drop it:**
The combinatorial resource-flow search produced 19,750 candidates, found **zero** real combos,
and generated **23** around a combo I already knew was broken. Root cause: every resource in the
model dangles (`RUNE_READY` 10 producers / 0 consumers) because the conversions that close a loop
(readied rune -> energy, deck -> hand, trash -> deck) were never modeled. Even repaired, real
combos are 4-5 cards (3e10 subsets) with game-state preconditions a static model can't express.

**What actually works:**
The 8 extraction agents, doing pure mechanical data transformation and never asked about combos,
spontaneously surfaced 7 combo findings — all 7 verified correct against card text, including
subtle refutations. Comprehension beat arithmetic.

**Revised architecture:**
```
full corpus (30k tok) + rules primer -> LLM proposes loops
                                     -> adversarial REFUTE pass (default: refuted)
                                     -> human review queue -> DB
```
No combinatorial search step. The whole card pool fits in one context window — use that.

## Critical Riftbound facts (do not re-derive, and do NOT port MTG intuitions)

- **NO life total.** "Infinite damage" is NOT a win condition. You win at **8 points**.
- Points: Conquer/Hold capped **once per battlefield per turn** (rule 470). Ability-granted
  points ("score/gain N point" on a card) are **UNCAPPED** — that's where infinite-point wins live.
- **Infinite draw is a LIABILITY** — decking yourself causes Burn Out, which gives an OPPONENT a point.
- Units **enter exhausted** (143.4). Gear enters ready.
- Rune Pool **empties** at start of Main Phase and end of turn (167) — resource floats are turn-scoped.
- Energy and Power are **different** resources; converting 1:1 is not a gain.
- **Domain Identity** is the color-identity analogue: `card.domains ⊆ legend.domains` (subset).
- `is_banned` must be **format-scoped and entity-typed** (cards, battlefields AND legends; 2v2 differs).

## Where things stand RIGHT NOW — Phase 0 is COMPLETE

The propose-then-refute hunt finished. Read `hunt.md` (37 KB) in the scratchpad for the detail.

**Precision: 48 candidates proposed -> 7 survived refutation (~15%).** That is a reviewable rate;
the pipeline works. But it produced a finding that matters far more than the number:

### THERE ARE NO INFINITE COMBOS IN THIS CARD POOL

Defended structurally, not just by failure to find one. I independently verified the first two:

1. **All 49 Legends have exactly 2 domains** (verified myself against `cards_full.json`).
   Since Domain Identity requires `card.domains ⊆ legend.domains`, any 3-domain combo is
   ILLEGAL. This kills many otherwise-working lines outright.
2. **Rune ramp is hard-capped at 12** — the Rune Deck is exactly 12 cards (161.2.a, 430.3).
   No ramp loop is possible in principle.
3. **No net-positive repeatable resource engine exists.** Every repeatable ability is gated by
   an exhaust, a "once each turn", a resource cost, or a Conquer/Hold trigger. The only ungated
   repeatable ready in the game is what **Dominus (VEN-142)** grants (`🔮🔮: Ready me`, 2 Power),
   while the best repeatable Add yields 1 Power, or 2 Power *plus a sacrificed permanent*
   (Malzahar). **Readying always costs >= what the readied thing produces.**

**Product implication — this is the big one.** LOOPLINE is built around infinite combos. Riftbound's
combo space is **MULTIPLICATIVE, not ITERATIVE**: value comes from stacking trigger-multipliers
(N copies of Ahri x K copies of Blue Sentinel on one Hold), not from loops. A straight LOOPLINE
port would be visualizing a category that barely exists here. **Re-examine the product concept
against this before building the graph UI.** Discuss with the user; do not silently redesign.

Surviving lines worth knowing (from `hunt.md`):
- **Ahri, Alluring (OGN-066) + Blue Sentinel (UNL-087) xK** — hold-trigger multiplication, 9-10 points
- **Tryndamere (OGN-034) + Red Brambleback (UNL-029) xK** — same axis on Conquer, mono-Fury
- **The Grand Plaza (OGN-293) + Recruit the Vanguard (OGS-015)** — 7+ units -> "you win the game" (195)
- **Gutter Palace (UNL-088)** — mono-Mind 1-card alternate win
- **Death from Below (UNL-186)** — the pool's only genuinely recursive spell (not Flow, so no banish)

Best refutations to learn the standard from:
- **Renata Glasc (SFD-088)** is the ONLY in-turn-repeatable "Score 1 point" in 920 cards. 8 activations
  = 32 Energy + 32 Mind Power + 7 readies against a ~12-resource ceiling. ~5x short.
- **Ekko, Recurrent (OGN-110)** Deathknell says **Recycle me** = bottom of DECK (416.5), while every
  reanimator reads *from your trash*. He puts himself out of reach of his own loop.
  ⚠️ CAVEAT I have not resolved: the documented Lux combo works *because the deck is empty*, so
  "bottom of deck" == "top of deck". The agent may have missed that precondition. Worth re-checking.
- **Pit Crew (OGN-091)** has the only free unlimited ready trigger — and no exhaust ability to spend it on.
