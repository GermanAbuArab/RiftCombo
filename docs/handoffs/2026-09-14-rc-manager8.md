## 3. My mistakes — do not repeat them

1. **I PROMOTED A RED SHA, AND MY OWN GATE HAD ALREADY SAID SO.** The script printed `exit 1` and
   `2 failed | 664 passed` and then ran `git push … :master` anyway, because the push was the next
   statement rather than a *consequence* of the result. **A gate whose output you have to read is a
   report; a gate that withholds the action is a gate.** The fixed form is in `CLAUDE.md`: five
   conditions, one `if`, and an explicit `GATE RED -> REFUSING TO PROMOTE` branch.
2. **I ALMOST SHIPPED THE DEFECT I HAD JUST DOCUMENTED.** A bullet ended up saying both "six checked,
   three hold" and "eight hold" — the partial-correction shape I had repaired in `combos.json` an hour
   earlier. **Amend a bullet in place; never append a second one about the same finding.**
3. **I over-counted a lane's citation gap 35 against its correct 16**, because 431.1.c covers *look at*
   **or** *reveal* and an entry arguing about a LOOK owes no 424 citation. The lane was right.
4. **I verified a push with `HEAD` instead of my own sha** — the exact trap I had been telling lanes
   about. `HEAD` is shared and moves; capture your sha right after your own commit and test THAT.
5. **I did not spawn my successor** until the user asked why. I wrote every durable record and stopped
   short of the one act that continues the work. **Preparing for a replacement is not creating one.**
