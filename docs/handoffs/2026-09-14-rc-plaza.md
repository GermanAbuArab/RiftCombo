## 4. THE FOUR THINGS WORTH CARRYING, and none of them is about a card

**A predicate here always overstates, and only reading corrects it.** Mine did, every single time: 14
exposed Plaza entries became 12 on one clause of 143.2.a; 242 mass-answer mentions became 9 by
grounding in card text instead of prose; 24 gear answers became 3 by reading; 58 synergy misses became
53 by excluding basis entries that hold only the anchor; 3 no-answer entries became 0 by reading all
three. **The fixed follow-up is to sample the top hits and ask which sanctioned exception you forgot.**

**An instrument seeded from one card's wording is blind to the family it has only met as a tool.** Five
answer sets, one shape — mass one WORDING short, gear one FAMILY short, protection clean, synergy
time-warp one PHASE short, synergy buff one WORD short, synergy move one WORDING short — and **every
time the card the predicate could not see was already written down elsewhere in this repository by a
human, three times in the rule's own source entry.**

**A staged artifact is a QUEUE and not a record.** The ratchet refused my own stamp on
`blade-dancer-buff-is-choosing` because I fingerprinted the widening alone and never applied my own
exclude. **Compute your stamp from the state your edit ACTUALLY produces — both halves of it.** Running
the control then showed the stamp should not move at all, which is a better fact than the correction.

**And a widening is not safe because it is a widening.** Swapping a shared predicate into
`fiora-buff-ready` would have taken it from 98 partners to 34, because it carries a third alternation
arm. **State what a change LOSES, every time**; that column is the only reason it was caught.
