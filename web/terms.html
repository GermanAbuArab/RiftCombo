What this is

RiftCombo is a free tool that reads a Riftbound deck list and tells you which catalogued combos it contains. It is a personal project, run by one person, with no advertising, no donations and nothing to buy. It can stop working or go away at any time.

What it claims

Every combo marked verified was walked by hand against Riot's card text and the Core Rules, with its sources attached; entries marked candidate were proposed and not yet reviewed. Card pairings shown under "Pairs in this deck" are text matches against a hand-verified rule, and the site says so where it shows them. All of it can still be wrong. At a table, the Core Rules and the head judge decide, not this site.

Your account and your lists

The decks you save are yours. Do not upload anything to this site that you would mind losing, and do not use it to store anything that is not a deck list. An account used to attack the site or to overload it will be removed.
