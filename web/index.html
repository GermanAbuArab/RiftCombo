Guide

  1. Paste a deck list (any common export), a deck code, or a public Piltover Archive link, then press Find combos.
  2. Complete shows combos your list already has every piece of. Near misses shows combos within a few cards, with the missing pieces outlined.
  3. What to add, under your list, prices every catalogued line that fits inside your legend's two domains and names the cheapest way to complete one. It never suggests a card your legend could not play.
  4. Pairs in this deck, below that, is a different kind of claim and says so. A combo is walked by hand card by card; a pair is a rule walked by hand once — "Blue Sentinel doubles any hold effect" — whose instances are then found by matching card text. Open one to read the rule, the Core Rules paragraphs behind it, and the verified combo it was taken from.
  5. Every combo carries a class, and the list is ordered by it — the ones that end the game come first. INFINITE was walked to a step that repeats with no bound on how often. BURST reaches the 8 points that win in a single scoring event. CHAIN reaches 8 across several scoring events with no turn of the opponent's in between. ALT WIN is a card that says outright that you win. ENGINE is repeatable advantage that does not close the game on its own — most of the catalogue, and the pieces the other four are built out of.
  6. Click a combo or a card to isolate it. The side panel lists the pieces, what has to be true before it works, the steps, and where each claim comes from.
  7. Click a card's picture in the side panel, or double-click a card in the diagram, to read it at full size with its rules text.
  8. My decks is your own library. Press New deck to write a list out, or paste a public Piltover Archive link to import one. Nothing is written to your account until you press Save or Update — there is no auto-save, and until then nothing about a list leaves your browser.
  9. Opening a deck shows the list on the left and Construction on the right: one row per deck-building rule, each citing the paragraph of Riot's Core Rules it stands on. A row marked unchecked is a rule this site can state but not verify from Riot's card data — the Signature cap is the one that matters, because the card gallery carries no Signature marker. Analyze combos hands the list to the Combos tab; Export deck code copies a code any Riftbound deck site reads.
  10. Combos marked candidate were proposed by an automated sweep and have not been walked by a human yet. Treat them as leads, not facts.

Sources

Card text and art come from Riot's official card gallery, with Riot's published errata applied. Legality follows the official Rules Hub. Combos are walked by hand against the Core Rules, card text and rule numbers cited. Entries marked candidate were proposed by an automated sweep and not yet reviewed. Pairs are a separate file with a weaker guarantee: the rule is walked once and cites its paragraphs, the instances are text matches, and every rule's whole match list was read card by card before it shipped.

Saving a deck stores the list text you typed, not the result of matching it, so an old list is read again against today's catalogue and today's ban list every time you open it. Only you can read your own rows.
