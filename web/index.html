How to use

  1. Paste a deck list (any common export), a deck code, or a public Piltover Archive link, then press Find combos.
  2. Included shows combos your list already contains. Near misses shows combos within a few cards, with the missing cards outlined.
  3. Click a route or a card to isolate it; the side panel lists cards, prerequisites, steps and sources.

Data & sources

Card text and art come from Riot's official card gallery, with Riot's published errata applied. Legality follows the official Rules Hub. Combos are hand-verified against the Core Rules; entries marked candidate were proposed and not yet reviewed.
