RiftCombo cross-references your list against a hand-verified database of Riftbound combos: unbounded loops, one-turn point bursts, and alternate wins. Each result names the cards you have, the cards you are missing, and whether the line is legal in your format.

RiftCombo was created under Riot Games' "Legal Jibber Jabber" policy using assets owned by Riot Games. Riot Games does not endorse or sponsor this project.

Card text from Riot's card gallery, with Riot's published errata applied. Legality from the official Rules Hub. Combos are hand-verified against the Core Rules; entries marked candidate are proposed and not yet reviewed.

No advertising, no donations, no play-rate or win-rate data. RiftCombo describes combos; it never plays them for you.
