Guide

  1. Paste a deck list (any common export), a deck code, or a public Piltover Archive link, then press Find combos.
  2. Complete shows combos your list already has every piece of. Near misses shows combos within a few cards, with the missing pieces outlined.
  3. What to add, under your list, prices every catalogued line that fits inside your legend's two domains and names the cheapest way to complete one. It never suggests a card your legend could not play.
  4. Pairs in this deck, below that, is a different kind of claim and says so. A combo is walked by hand card by card; a pair is a rule walked by hand once — "Blue Sentinel doubles any hold effect" — whose instances are then found by matching card text. Open one to read the rule, the Core Rules paragraphs behind it, and the verified combo it was taken from.
  5. Click a combo or a card to isolate it. The side panel lists the pieces, what has to be true before it works, the steps, and where each claim comes from.
  6. Click a card's picture in the side panel, or double-click a card in the diagram, to read it at full size with its rules text.
  7. Combos marked candidate were proposed by an automated sweep and have not been walked by a human yet. Treat them as leads, not facts.

Sources

Card text and art come from Riot's official card gallery, with Riot's published errata applied. Legality follows the official Rules Hub. Combos are walked by hand against the Core Rules; entries marked candidate were proposed and not yet reviewed. Pairs are a separate file with a weaker guarantee: the rule is walked once and cites its paragraphs, the instances are text matches, and every rule's whole match list was read card by card before it shipped.
